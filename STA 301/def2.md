### Lecture 10 – Measures of Dispersion

**1. Concept of Dispersion**

Dispersion ka matlab hai data ke numbers kitne phailay huay hain ya aik doosray se kitne door hain.

Example:

Group A: 48, 49, 50, 51, 52

Group B: 10, 30, 50, 70, 90

Group B ka data zyada phaila hua hai.

**2. Absolute Measures of Dispersion**

Data ke spread ko asal units mein measure karna Absolute Measures of Dispersion kehlata hai.

**3. Relative Measures of Dispersion**

Data ke spread ko ratio ya percentage ki form mein compare karna Relative Measures of Dispersion kehlata hai.

**4. Range**

Sab se baray number mein se sab se chhota number minus karna Range kehlata hai.

Formula:

Range = Highest Value − Lowest Value

Example:

10, 20, 30, 40

Range = 40 − 10 = 30

**5. Coefficient of Dispersion**

Dispersion ko ratio ki form mein show karna Coefficient of Dispersion kehlata hai.

**6. Quartile Deviation**

Third Quartile aur First Quartile ke difference ka aadha Quartile Deviation kehlata hai.

Formula:

QD = (Q3 − Q1) / 2

**7. Coefficient of Quartile Deviation**

Quartile Deviation ko relative form mein show karna Coefficient of Quartile Deviation kehlata hai.

Formula:

(Q3 − Q1) / (Q3 + Q1)

### Lecture 11 – Mean Deviation, Standard Deviation aur Variance

**1. Mean Deviation**

Har number aur central value ke darmiyan distance ka average Mean Deviation kehlata hai.

Simple words: Numbers apne average se kitne door hain.

**2. Standard Deviation (SD)**

Standard Deviation batata hai ke data ke numbers Mean se kitne door ya phailay huay hain.

- SD kam ho = Numbers aik doosray ke qareeb hain.
- SD zyada ho = Numbers zyada phailay huay hain.

**3. Variance**

Standard Deviation ka square Variance kehlata hai.

Formula:

Variance = (Standard Deviation)²

**4. Coefficient of Variation (CV)**

Data ke spread ko percentage mein show karna Coefficient of Variation kehlata hai.

Formula:

CV = (SD / Mean) × 100

### Lecture 12 – Chebyshev's Inequality, Empirical Rule aur Five Number Summary

**1. Chebyshev's Inequality**

Ye rule batata hai ke kisi bhi distribution mein data ka kam az kam kitna hissa Mean se ek certain distance ke andar hota hai.

Formula:

Minimum proportion = 1 − (1 / k²)

Yahan k ka matlab Standard Deviations ki tadaad hai aur k > 1 hona chahiye.

**2. Empirical Rule**

Ye rule Normal Distribution ke liye hota hai.

- 68% data = Mean se 1 SD ke andar.
- 95% data = Mean se 2 SD ke andar.
- 99.7% data = Mean se 3 SD ke andar.

**3. Five Number Summary**

Data ko summarize karne ke liye 5 important values use hoti hain:

1. Minimum = Sab se chhoti value.
2. Q1 = First Quartile.
3. Median = Beech wali value.
4. Q3 = Third Quartile.
5. Maximum = Sab se bari value.

### Lecture 13 – Box Plot aur Data Skewness

**1. Box Plot**

Box Plot aik graph hota hai jo data ka spread, Median aur Quartiles show karta hai.

Is mein 5 important values use hoti hain:

- Minimum
- Q1
- Median
- Q3
- Maximum

**2. Data Skewness**

Skewness batati hai ke data ki distribution kis taraf jhuki hui hai.

Iski 3 basic types hain:

**Positive Skewness (Right Skewed)**

Jab graph ki tail right side ki taraf lambi ho.

**Negative Skewness (Left Skewed)**

Jab graph ki tail left side ki taraf lambi ho.

**Symmetrical Distribution**

Jab graph dono sides se lag bhag aik jaisa ho.
