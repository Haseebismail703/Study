1. Observations ⭐
An observation is a recorded piece of information.

Book ke examples:
- Physical measurement: Height or weight.
- Classification: Heads or tails.
- Answer to a question: Yes or no.

2. Variables
A variable is a characteristic that can change from one person or object to another.
Examples: Age, height, weight.

1) Quantitative Variable ⭐
A quantitative variable is a characteristic that can be expressed in numbers.
Age
Weight
Income
Number of children

2) Qualitative Variable ⭐
A variable with a non-numerical characteristic is called a qualitative variable.
Education
Sex
Eye colour
Quality


Discrete and Continuous Variables
1) Discrete
A discrete variable takes separate, countable values, usually whole numbers.
Number of persons in a family
Number of rooms in a house
Number of deaths in an accident

2) Continuous Variables
A continuous variable can take whole-number and fractional values within an interval.
Height of a plant
Weight of a commodity
