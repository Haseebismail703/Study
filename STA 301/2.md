# STA301 — Lesson 2: Types of Data and Sampling
## Short Revision Notes

## 1. Types of Data

### Primary Data

**Book Definition:** Data originally collected as raw data, which have not undergone any sort of statistical treatment, are called primary data.

**Easy Definition:** Data collected for the first time without statistical treatment.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab researcher khud apni study ke liye pehli baar data collect karta hai, to usay primary data kehte hain.

**Example:** Students se unki age pooch kar data collect karna.

### Secondary Data

**Book Definition:** Data that have undergone statistical treatment at least once are called secondary data.

**Easy Definition:** Data that have already undergone statistical treatment.

**Roman Urdu:** Aisa data jis par pehle statistical treatment ho chuka ho.

**Example:** Kisi published statistical report se data lena.

## 2. Methods of Data Collection

### Collection of Primary Data

1. **Direct Personal Investigation:** Researcher khud logon se information collect karta hai.
2. **Indirect Investigation:** Researcher doosre logon se information hasil karta hai.
3. **Questionnaires:** Sawalat ki list ke zariye answers collect kiye jate hain.
4. **Through Enumerators:** Trained persons logon se sawalat pooch kar data collect karte hain.
5. **Local Sources:** Local logon ya informants se information hasil ki jati hai.

### Collection of Secondary Data

1. **Official Publications:** Government departments ki reports.
2. **Semi-official Sources:** Aise idaron ki reports, jaise State Bank of Pakistan.
3. **Trade Associations:** Business aur trade organizations ka data.
4. **Journals and Newspapers:** Published articles aur reports.
5. **Research Organizations and Universities:** Research reports aur studies.

## 3. Population

**Book Definition:** A collection of all the members of a group possessing the same basic and defined characteristics is called a population.

**Easy Definition:** The complete group of people or items being studied.

**Roman Urdu:** Study mein shamil poora group population kehlata hai.

**Example:** College ke tamam 1,000 students.

### Types of Population

- **Finite Population:** A population with a limited number of elements.
  - Example: College ke 1,000 students.

- **Infinite Population:** A population considered unlimited in size.
  - Example: A process involving an unlimited number of possible observations, such as repeated die throws.

### Target Population

**Easy Definition:** The complete population about which information is required.

**Roman Urdu:** Woh poora group jis ke bare mein researcher information hasil karna chahta hai.

### Sampled Population

**Easy Definition:** The population from which the sample is actually selected.

**Roman Urdu:** Woh group jis mein se asal mein sample select kiya jata hai.

## 4. Sample, Sampling and Census

- **Sample:** A part of the population selected for study.
- **Sampling:** The process of selecting a sample from a population.
- **Census:** Collecting information from every member of the population.

**Example:** College mein 1,000 students hain:
- Population = 1,000 students
- Sample = Selected 100 students
- Sampling = Un 100 students ko select karne ka process
- Census = Tamam 1,000 students se information collect karna

## 5. Sampling Frame

**Book Definition:** A sampling frame is a complete list of all the elements in the population.

**Easy Definition:** A list of all population members from which a sample can be selected.

**Roman Urdu:** Population ke tamam members ki list, jis mein se sample select kiya jata hai.

**Example:** College ke tamam students ki registration list.

## 6. Sampling and Non-Sampling Errors

### Sampling Error

**Easy Definition:** The difference between a sample result and the true population value.

**Roman Urdu:** Sample se milne wali value aur poori population ki asal value ke darmiyan farq.

**Example:** Population ki average height 165 cm hai, lekin sample ki average height 162 cm hai. Dono results mein farq sampling error ko show karta hai.

### Non-Sampling Error

**Easy Definition:** Errors caused by problems in collecting or recording data.

**Roman Urdu:** Data collect karte, record karte ya report karte waqt hone wali ghaltiyan.

**Main Sources:**
1. Defective sampling frame.
2. Incorrect reporting of information.
3. Negligence of investigators.
4. Non-response to questionnaires.

**How to Reduce Non-Sampling Errors:**
1. Follow up on non-responses.
2. Train investigators properly.
3. Check collected information carefully.

## 7. Types of Sampling

### Random Sampling

**Easy Definition:** A method in which sample members are selected by chance.

**Roman Urdu:** Members ko chance ya lottery ke zariye select kiya jata hai.

**Example:** Students ke names ki parchi nikal kar select karna.

### Non-Random Sampling

**Easy Definition:** A method in which sample members are selected using personal judgment rather than chance.

**Roman Urdu:** Researcher apni choice ya judgment se members select karta hai.

### Quota Sampling

**Easy Definition:** A non-random method in which people are selected according to fixed quotas.

**Roman Urdu:** Researcher ko specific categories aur numbers diye jate hain, aur woh unke mutabiq log select karta hai.

**Example:** Study ke liye ek fixed number mein different categories ke log select karna.

## Important Differences

| Random Sampling | Non-Random Sampling |
|---|---|
| Selection by chance. | Selection by personal judgment. |
| Example: Simple random sampling. | Example: Quota sampling. |

| Sampling Error | Non-Sampling Error |
|---|---|
| Sample result aur population value ka farq. | Data collection ya recording ki ghalti. |
| Sample selection se related. | Reporting, response ya investigation se related. |

## Important Quiz Points

- Primary data: Originally collected raw data.
- Secondary data: Data that have undergone statistical treatment.
- Population: Complete group under study.
- Sample: A part of the population.
- Sampling: The process of selecting a sample.
- Census: Data collection from every member of a population.
- Sampling frame: Complete list of population elements.
- Random sampling: Selection by chance.
- Non-random sampling: Selection by personal judgment.
- Quota sampling: A type of non-random sampling.
- Sampling error: Difference between a sample result and the true population value.
- Non-sampling errors can occur even in a census.
