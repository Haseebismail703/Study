### Lecture 7 – Arithmetic Mean, Weighted Mean aur Median

**1. Arithmetic Mean (Normal Average)**

Tamam numbers ko jama karke total numbers ki tadaad se divide karna Arithmetic Mean kehlata hai.

Example:

10, 20, 30

Mean = (10 + 20 + 30) / 3 = 20

**2. Weighted Mean**

Jab har number ki importance ya weight alag ho aur average weight ke mutabiq nikala jaye to usay Weighted Mean kehte hain.

Example:

Midterm = 80 marks, weight = 40%

Final = 60 marks, weight = 60%

Weighted Mean = (80 × 0.40) + (60 × 0.60) = 68

**3. Median (Ungrouped Data)**

Numbers ko chhotay se baray order mein arrange karne ke baad beech wala number Median kehlata hai.

- Odd numbers hon to beech wala number lete hain.
- Even numbers hon to beech ke 2 numbers ka average nikalte hain.

Example:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

Median = 30

### Lecture 8 – Grouped Median, Empirical Relation aur Quantiles

**1. Median in Grouped Data**

Jab data frequency table ki form mein diya ho aur individual numbers alag alag na diye hon to us mein Median calculate karna Grouped Median kehlata hai.

**2. Open-Ended Frequency Distribution**

Jab frequency table ki pehli ya aakhri class ki boundary mukammal na di gayi ho to usay Open-Ended Frequency Distribution kehte hain.

Example:

Below 10, 10–20, 20–30, 30 and above.

**3. Empirical Relation Between Mean, Median and Mode**

Ye Mean, Median aur Mode ke darmiyan aik approximate relationship hai.

Formula:

Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean

**4. Quantiles**

Quantiles data ko barabar hisson mein divide karne ka tareeqa hain.

- Quartiles: Data ko 4 barabar hisson mein divide karte hain.
- Deciles: Data ko 10 barabar hisson mein divide karte hain.
- Percentiles: Data ko 100 barabar hisson mein divide karte hain.

**5. Graphic Location of Quantiles**

Graph ki madad se Quartiles, Deciles aur Percentiles ki position maloom karna Graphic Location of Quantiles kehlata hai.

### Lecture 9 – Geometric Mean aur Harmonic Mean

**1. Geometric Mean (GM)**

Numbers ko multiply karke unka nth root nikalna Geometric Mean kehlata hai.

Example:

4 aur 16

GM = √(4 × 16) = 8

**2. Harmonic Mean (HM)**

Numbers ke reciprocals ka average nikal kar uska reciprocal lena Harmonic Mean kehlata hai.

Example:

2 aur 4

HM = 2 / (1/2 + 1/4) = 2.67 (approximately)

**3. Relation Between AM, GM and HM**

Arithmetic Mean, Geometric Mean aur Harmonic Mean ka aapas mein relationship hota hai.

Positive numbers ke liye:

AM ≥ GM ≥ HM

**4. Some Other Measures of Central Tendency**

Mean, Median aur Mode ke ilawa bhi data ka central point maloom karne ke kuch doosray tareeqay hote hain.
