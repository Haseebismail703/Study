# STA301 — Lesson 3: Qualitative Data

## Qualitative Data

**Easy English Definition:** Qualitative data describe categories or characteristics.

**Roman Urdu:** Aisa data jo categories bataye, jaise students ka medium of education.

### Qualitative Data is divided into:

1. **Univariate Frequency Table**
2. **Bivariate Frequency Table**

### 1. Univariate Frequency Table

**Easy English Definition:** A table showing the frequency of each category of one variable.

**Example from the lecture:** Medium of institution.

| Medium | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Urdu | 719 |
| English | 481 |
| Total | 1200 |

**Roman Urdu:** Yahan ek variable hai, yani medium of institution. Table batati hai ke kitne students Urdu aur English medium se hain.

#### A. Percentages

**Easy English Definition:** A percentage expresses a category's frequency as a part of 100.

**Formula:**

Percentage = (Category Frequency / Total Frequency) × 100

**Example: Urdu medium**

(719 / 1200) × 100 = 59.92%

**Roman Urdu:** Category ki frequency ko total frequency se divide karke 100 se multiply karte hain.

#### B. Pie Chart

**Definition — Easy English:** A pie chart represents categories as sectors of a circle, showing each category's proportion of the total.

**Roman Urdu:** Pie chart ek circle hota hai. Har category ko circle ka ek hissa milta hai, jo total mein us category ka proportion show karta hai.

**Formula to calculate the angle:**

Angle = (Category Frequency / Total Frequency) × 360°

**Example: Urdu medium**

Angle = (719 / 1200) × 360°

Angle = 215.7° (approximately 216°)

**Example: English medium**

Angle = (481 / 1200) × 360°

Angle = 144.3° (approximately 144°)

**Check:** Dono angles ka total 360° hona chahiye.

**MCQ clues:**
- Circle divided into sectors → Pie Chart
- Angle of a sector → Frequency / Total Frequency × 360°
- Percentage → Frequency / Total Frequency × 100

#### C. Bar Chart

**Easy English Definition:** A bar chart represents categories using separate bars whose heights or lengths show their frequencies.

**Roman Urdu:** Har category ke liye alag bar hoti hai. Bar ki height ya length frequency ko show karti hai.

**MCQ clue:** Separate bars showing category frequencies → Bar Chart.

### 2. Bivariate Frequency Table

**Easy English Definition:** A table showing frequencies for two variables together.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab do variables ka data ek table mein diya ho.

#### A. Component Bar Chart

**Easy English Definition:** A bar chart in which each bar is divided into components of a total.

**Roman Urdu:** Ek bar ko parts mein divide kiya jata hai aur har part total ka hissa hota hai.

**MCQ clue:** One bar divided into components → Component Bar Chart.

#### B. Multiple Bar Chart

**Easy English Definition:** A chart using separate bars side by side to compare groups.

**Roman Urdu:** Mukhtalif groups ka comparison karne ke liye alag bars side by side hoti hain.

**MCQ clue:** Side-by-side bars for comparison → Multiple Bar Chart.

## Important Difference: Component vs. Multiple Bar Chart

| Component Bar Chart | Multiple Bar Chart |
|---|---|
| One bar is subdivided into parts. | Bars are grouped together. |
| Used for totals and their components. | Used to compare different values or information. |
| Parts add up to the total. | Compared values do not necessarily add up to one total. |

## Quick Exam Revision

- **Qualitative variable:** A non-numerical characteristic; also called an attribute.
- **Univariate:** One variable.
- **Bivariate:** Two variables together.
- **Percentage formula:** Frequency / Total Frequency × 100.
- **Pie chart angle:** Frequency / Total Frequency × 360°.
- **Pie chart:** A circle divided into parts for distinct categories.
- **Simple bar chart:** Horizontal or vertical bars of equal width.
- **Component bar chart:** A subdivided bar showing components of a total.
- **Multiple bar chart:** Grouped bars used to compare values.
- **VU handout example:** Urdu = 719, English = 481, Total = 1200. The pie chart angles are 215.7° and 144.3°, which add up to 360°.

## Final Revision

- **Univariate Frequency Table:** One variable.
- **Percentages:** Multiply the frequency-to-total ratio by 100.
- **Pie Chart angle:** Multiply the frequency-to-total ratio by 360°.
- **Bar Chart:** Separate bars.
- **Bivariate Frequency Table:** Two variables together.
- **Component Bar Chart:** One bar divided into components.
- **Multiple Bar Chart:** Separate bars side by side.
