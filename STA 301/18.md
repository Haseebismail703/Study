## Definitions of Probability

### 1. Probability

**Definition:** Probability is the chance of an event happening.

**Roman Urdu:** Kisi event ke hone ke chance ko Probability kehte hain.

**Example:**
- Head aane ka chance = 1/2
- Tail aane ka chance = 1/2

### 2. Subjective Probability

**Definition:** Probability based on personal belief or opinion.

**Roman Urdu:** Apni soch ya opinion ke mutabiq kisi result ka chance batana.

**Example:** I think Pakistan will win the match.

### 3. Objective Probability

**Definition:** Probability based on facts or mathematical methods.

**Roman Urdu:** Facts ya calculations ki madad se probability nikalna.

**Types:**
1. Classical Probability
2. Relative Frequency Probability

### 4. Classical Probability

**Definition:** Probability is the ratio of favourable outcomes to total possible outcomes.

**Roman Urdu:** Favourable results ko total possible results se divide karna.

**Formula:**

P(A) = m / n

- m = Number of favourable outcomes
- n = Total possible outcomes

**Example:**

A fair coin is tossed once. Find the probability of getting Head.

Step 1: Sample Space

S = {H, T}

Step 2: Total outcomes

n = 2

Step 3: Favourable outcomes

m = 1

Step 4: Formula

P(A) = m / n

Step 5: Put values

P(H) = 1 / 2 = 0.5

**Answer = 0.5 or 50%**

### 5. Relative Frequency Probability

**Definition:** Probability based on the results of an experiment repeated many times.

**Roman Urdu:** Experiment ko bohat dafa repeat karke kisi result ke aane ki frequency se probability nikalna.

**Formula:**

P(A) = Number of times event occurs / Total number of trials

- Event occurs = Event jitni dafa aya
- Total trials = Experiment jitni dafa repeat hua

**Example:**

A coin is tossed 100 times. Head appears 60 times. Find the probability of Head.

Step 1: Given values

Total trials = 100

Head appears = 60

Step 2: Formula

P(A) = Event occurs / Total trials

Step 3: Put values

P(H) = 60 / 100

P(H) = 0.6

**Answer = 0.6 or 60%**

---

## Important Difference

| Classical Probability | Relative Frequency |
|---|---|
| Mathematical calculation | Actual experiment |
| Possible outcomes | Repeated trials |
| Favourable / Total possible | Occurrences / Total trials |

## Important Formulas – Revision

1. Classical Probability:

P(A) = m / n

2. Relative Frequency Probability:

P(A) = Number of times event occurs / Total number of trials

3. Probability range:

0 ≤ P(A) ≤ 1

- 0 = Impossible event
- 1 = Certain event
- 0.5 = 50% chance

## Important Keywords

- Probability = Chance
- Subjective = Personal opinion
- Objective = Facts and calculations
- Classical = Favourable outcomes
- Relative Frequency = Repeated experiment
- Trial = One performance of an experiment
