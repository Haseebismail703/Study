# CS101 – Module 39 & 40

## Module 39: Data Manipulation: Rotation and Shift

### 1. Shift Operation

**Definition:** Shift moves bits to the left or right. The removed bit is discarded and the empty position is filled with 0.

**Roman Urdu:** Binary bits ko left ya right move karna Shift kehlata hai. Jo bit bahar nikalti hai woh delete ho jati hai aur empty jagah par 0 aa jata hai.

#### A. Right Shift

Bits move to the right.

```text
Original: 10110010
Right:    01011001
```

- Rightmost bit is discarded.
- Left side is filled with 0.

#### B. Left Shift

Bits move to the left.

```text
Original: 10110010
Left:     01100100
```

- Leftmost bit is discarded.
- Right side is filled with 0.

### 2. Circular Shift (Rotation)

**Definition:** Circular shift moves the removed bit to the opposite end.

**Roman Urdu:** Jo bit aik side se bahar nikalti hai, woh doosri side par wapas aa jati hai.

#### A. Right Circular Shift

```text
Original: 10110010
Rotate:   01011001
```

Rightmost bit returns to the leftmost position.

#### B. Left Circular Shift

```text
Original: 10110010
Rotate:   01100101
```

Leftmost bit returns to the rightmost position.

**Important:**
- Circular Shift = Bit returns to opposite end.
- Logical Shift = Removed bit is discarded.
- 8-bit register rotated 8 times returns to its original pattern.

### 3. Arithmetic Shift

**Definition:** Arithmetic shift preserves the sign bit during a right shift.

**Roman Urdu:** Negative ya positive number ka sign bit change nahi hone dete.

**Important Points:**
- Sign bit = Leftmost bit.
- 0 = Positive.
- 1 = Negative (two's complement).
- Arithmetic right shift preserves the sign bit.

### 4. Uses of Shift Operations

- Left Shift = Multiplication by 2.
- Right Shift = Division by 2.
- Circular Shift = Rotating bits.
- Arithmetic Shift = Preserving sign bit.

## Module 40: Data Manipulation: Arithmetic Operators

### 1. Subtraction

**Definition:** Subtraction can be performed using addition and negation.

**Roman Urdu:** Computer subtraction ko addition ki madad se perform kar sakta hai. Iske liye negative number use hota hai.

**Formula:**

Subtraction = Addition + Negation

**Example:**

7 - 5 = 7 + (-5) = 2

### 2. Two's Complement

**Definition:** Two's complement is used to represent positive and negative integers.

**Roman Urdu:** Computer mein positive aur negative whole numbers represent karne ke liye Two's Complement use hota hai.

**Example (4-bit):**

| Decimal | Binary |
|---|---|
| +7 | 0111 |
| -5 | 1011 |

Addition:

```text
  0111
+ 1011
------
  0010
```

Answer = +2

### 3. Multiplication

**Definition:** Multiplication is repeated addition.

**Roman Urdu:** Aik number ko baar baar add karna multiplication kehlata hai.

**Example:**

8 × 3 = 8 + 8 + 8 = 24

**Important:** Multiplication = Repeated Addition.

### 4. Division

**Definition:** Division can be performed through repeated subtraction.

**Roman Urdu:** Aik number ko baar baar subtract karna division perform karne ka aik tareeqa hai.

**Example:**

12 ÷ 3

```text
12 - 3 = 9
 9 - 3 = 6
 6 - 3 = 3
 3 - 3 = 0
```

Total = 4 times

Answer = 4

**Important:** Division = Repeated Subtraction.

### 5. Small CPUs

Some small CPUs use only addition or addition and subtraction to perform arithmetic operations.

**Roman Urdu:** Kuch small CPUs mein sirf addition ya addition aur subtraction hoti hai. In operations ki madad se doosri calculations bhi ki ja sakti hain.

### 6. Floating Point Notation

**Definition:** Floating point notation represents numbers using significant digits and an exponent.

**Roman Urdu:** Decimal numbers ko computer mein represent karne ke liye Floating Point Notation use hoti hai.

**Important Components:**

- **Mantissa:** Stores significant digits.
- **Exponent:** Represents the scale or position of the number.
- **Sign Bit:** Shows positive or negative.

**Remember:** Floating Point = Mantissa + Exponent + Sign Bit.
