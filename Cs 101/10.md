# CS101 — Lesson 10: Roots of Computing
## Short Revision Notes

### 1. Abacus

**Definition:** An abacus is an early device used for counting and calculations.

- **Use:** Counting and calculations.
- **Important:** One of the earliest computing devices.

### 2. Technology of Gears

**Definition:** Gears are mechanical parts used to transfer movement in machines.

**Important Names and Years:**

- **Blaise Pascal (1623–1662):** Developed Pascaline, a mechanical machine used for performing basic calculations.
- **Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz (1646–1716):** Developed mechanical calculating machines that used gears to perform arithmetic operations.
- **Charles Babbage (1792–1871):** Designed mechanical calculating machines and is known as the Father of the Computer.

### 3. Punch Cards

**Definition:** Punch cards are paper cards that represent information using holes.

**Important Name and Year:**

- **Herman Hollerith (1860–1929):** Used punch cards to process census data efficiently in the 1890 US Census.
- **1890:** Punch-card technology was used for the US Census.
- Hollerith's work contributed to the history of IBM.

### 4. Mechanical and Electronic Computers

**Mechanical Computer:** Uses mechanical parts, such as gears, to perform operations.

**Electronic Computer:** Uses electronic components to perform calculations.

**Important Names and Years:**

- **George Stibitz (1940):** Developed an electromechanical calculating machine at Bell Laboratories.
- **Howard Aiken (1944):** Led the development of Harvard Mark I with IBM engineers.

### 5. ENIAC

**Full Form:** Electronic Numerical Integrator and Calculator.

**Definition:** ENIAC was an early electronic computer developed for performing calculations.

**Important Names:**

- **John Mauchly:** Co-developed ENIAC, an early electronic computer, with J. Presper Eckert.
- **J. Presper Eckert:** Co-developed ENIAC with John Mauchly at the University of Pennsylvania.

**Important Facts:**

- Vacuum tubes: 20,000
- Weight: 30 tons
- Electricity: 200 kilowatts
- Cost: $487,000

### 6. Rapid Advancement

**Definition:** Rapid advancement means the fast development and improvement of computer technology.

**Important Names and Years:**

- **Steve Jobs (1976):** Co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Wozniak.
- **Steve Wozniak (1976):** Co-founded Apple Computer and designed early Apple computers.
- **1981:** IBM launched its IBM PC.
