
# ENG101 — Lecture 7: Word Formation
## Short Exam Revision Notes

### 1. Stem
**Definition:** The main part of a word to which affixes can be added.

**Roman Urdu:** Word ka main hissa jis ke saath prefix ya suffix lag sakta hai.

**Example:** act → react → action

### 2. Prefix
**Definition:** A word part added at the beginning of a word.

**Roman Urdu:** Prefix word ke start mein lagta hai aur aksar meaning change karta hai.

**Examples:**
- happy → unhappy
- act → react
- possible → impossible

### 3. Suffix
**Definition:** A word part added at the end of a word.

**Roman Urdu:** Suffix word ke end mein lagta hai. Yeh meaning ya word ki type change kar sakta hai.

**Examples:**
- kind → kindness
- develop → development
- quick → quickly

### 4. Affixes
**Definition:** Prefixes and suffixes are called affixes.

**Roman Urdu:** Prefix aur suffix dono ko milakar affixes kehte hain.

### 5. Word Formation
**Definition:** Word formation is the process of creating new words.

**Roman Urdu:** Prefix, suffix ya doosre tareeqon se naye words banana word formation hai.

**Example:** happy → happiness

## Parts of Speech and Suffixes

### 6. Noun-forming Suffixes
**Definition:** Suffixes that form nouns from other words.

**Roman Urdu:** Aise suffixes jo noun banate hain.

- **-ness:** kind → kindness
- **-tion:** educate → education
- **-ment:** develop → development

### 7. Verb-forming Suffixes
**Definition:** Suffixes that form verbs from other words.

**Roman Urdu:** Aise suffixes jo action ya kaam batane wala verb banate hain.

- **-ize:** modern → modernize
- **-ify:** simple → simplify
- **-en:** short → shorten

### 8. Adjective-forming Suffixes
**Definition:** Suffixes that form adjectives from other words.

**Roman Urdu:** Aise suffixes jo quality batane wala adjective banate hain.

- **-able:** read → readable
- **-ful:** care → careful
- **-less:** care → careless
- **-ous:** danger → dangerous

### 9. Adverb-forming Suffixes
**Definition:** Suffixes that form adverbs from other words.

**Roman Urdu:** Aise suffixes jo batate hain ke kaam kaise hua.

- **-ly:** quick → quickly
- **-ly:** slow → slowly

## Spelling Changes When Adding Suffixes

### 10. Adding a Suffix
**Rule:** Sometimes a suffix is added without changing the original word.

**Roman Urdu:** Kabhi original word mein koi letter change nahi hota.

- care + ful = careful
- develop + ment = development

### 11. Removing Final -e
**Rule:** Final -e is often dropped before a suffix beginning with a vowel.

**Roman Urdu:** Kuch words mein suffix lagate waqt aakhri e remove hota hai.

- make + ing = making
- create + ive = creative

### 12. Changing -y to -i
**Rule:** A consonant + y often changes to i before certain suffixes.

**Roman Urdu:** Consonant ke baad y ho to kuch suffixes lagane par y, i ban jata hai.

- happy + ness = happiness
- easy + ly = easily

**Exception:** cry + ing = crying

### 13. Doubling the Final Consonant
**Rule:** Some short words double their final consonant before a suffix.

**Roman Urdu:** Kuch words mein suffix lagate waqt aakhri consonant double hota hai.

- run + ing = running
- sit + ing = sitting
- big + er = bigger

## Exam Tips

- Prefix = beginning of a word.
- Stem = main part of a word.
- Suffix = end of a word.
- Affixes = prefixes and suffixes.
- Noun = name, thing, person, or idea.
- Verb = action or state.
- Adjective = describes a noun.
- Adverb = often describes a verb.
- Suffixes can change a word's meaning or grammatical category.
- Spelling changes depend on the word and suffix; the rules have exceptions.
