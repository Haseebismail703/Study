# ENG101 – Lecture 16
## Reading: Evaluating Texts – Writer's Tone & Intention

### 1. Evaluating Texts

**Definition:** Evaluating a text means understanding the writer's intention, point of view, tone and possible bias.

**Roman Urdu:** Kisi passage ko parh kar samajhna ke writer kya kehna chahta hai, uski soch kya hai aur woh kis attitude se baat kar raha hai.

**Important points:**
- Writer's intention
- Writer's point of view
- Writer's tone
- Writer's bias
- Facts and opinions
- Arguments and supporting evidence

### 2. Writer's Tone

**Definition:** Tone is the writer's attitude towards a subject or topic.

**Roman Urdu:** Writer kisi topic ke bare mein kis jazbaat ya attitude se likh raha hai, usay tone kehte hain.

#### Important Types of Tone

| Tone | Roman Urdu Meaning |
|---|---|
| Optimistic | Umeed rakhne wala |
| Pessimistic | Mayoos soch rakhne wala |
| Bitter | Narazgi ya talkhi |
| Tolerant | Bardasht karne wala |
| Sentimental | Jazbati |
| Humorous | Mazahiya |
| Sympathetic | Hamdardi rakhne wala |
| Angry | Ghusse wala |
| Critical | Tanqeed karne wala |
| Ironic | Kehna kuch aur, matlab kuch aur |

**Handout examples:**

1. Sentimental: Bachpan ka ghar purana hai lekin dil ke qareeb hai.
2. Tolerant: Ghar acha nahi hai, lekin itna bura bhi nahi.
3. Bitter: Agar meri achi job hoti to mujhe is kharab ghar mein nahi rehna parta.
4. Optimistic: Umeed hai landlord jald ghar ki marammat karwa dega.
5. Humorous: Ghar chortay waqt main is mein doosron ka kachra bhi daal jaunga.

### 3. Irony

**Definition:** Irony occurs when there is a difference between what is expected and what actually happens, or between what is said and what is meant.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab baat kuch kahi jaye lekin matlab kuch aur ho, ya jo umeed ho uske bilkul ulat ho, to irony kehte hain.

Irony ki 2 important types hain:

#### A. Situational Irony

**Definition:** Situational irony occurs when something unexpected happens instead of what is expected.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab kisi situation mein hum kuch aur expect kar rahe hon lekin uske bilkul ulat ho jaye.

**Example:** Firefighter aag bujhane ke liye pani ke bajaye petrol daal de.

#### B. Verbal Irony

**Definition:** Verbal irony occurs when a person says something but means the opposite.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab insan kuch bolta hai lekin uska asal matlab uske ulat hota hai.

**Example:** Cricket match ke din barish ho rahi ho aur player kahe:

"Oh, great weather!"

Asal mein woh kharab weather ki baat kar raha hai.

**Difference:**

| Situational Irony | Verbal Irony |
|---|---|
| Situation unexpected hoti hai | Words ka matlab opposite hota hai |
| Expectation aur reality mein difference | Saying aur meaning mein difference |

### 4. Argument

**Definition:** A good argument is one in which a writer makes a point and provides supporting evidence to prove it.

**Roman Urdu:** Writer pehle apni baat ya claim karta hai aur phir usay sabit karne ke liye reasons aur examples deta hai.

Argument ke 2 parts:

**A. Point:** Main baat ya claim jo writer kehna chahta hai.

**B. Support:** Evidence, reasons ya examples jo point ko prove karte hain.

**Example:**

Point: My neighbours are inconsiderate.

Support:
- They play loud music at night.
- Their children scream outside.
- Their dog barks all day.

Yaad rakho:
- Point = Main baat
- Support = Saboot

### 5. Types of Fallacies

**Definition:** A fallacy is an error in reasoning or an incorrect argument.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab koi insan ghalat logic ya kamzor daleel se apni baat prove karne ki koshish kare to usay fallacy kehte hain.

Fallacies ki 2 main categories hain:

### A. Fallacies That Ignore the Issue

Is category mein writer asal topic ko ignore karta hai.

Iski 3 types hain:

#### 1. Changing the Subject

**Definition:** Diverting attention from the actual issue to an unrelated topic.

**Roman Urdu:** Asal topic se hat kar kisi doosri ghair mutaliq baat par discussion karna.

Example: Kisi leader ki performance poochi jaye aur jawab mein uski family ki tareef shuru kar di jaye.

#### 2. Circular Reasoning

**Definition:** Using the same idea as both the reason and the conclusion.

**Roman Urdu:** Apni baat prove karne ke liye wohi baat dobara reason ke taur par kehna.

Example: Ali is a great teacher because he teaches very well.

Yahan koi naya saboot nahi diya gaya.

#### 3. Personal Attack

**Definition:** Attacking a person's character instead of discussing the actual issue.

**Roman Urdu:** Asal baat ka jawab dene ke bajaye kisi insan ki zaati buraiyan shuru kar dena.

Example: Kisi ke tax proposal ka jawab dene ke bajaye kehna ke woh khud tax nahi deta.

### B. Fallacies That Overgeneralize / Oversimplify Issues

Is category mein writer kam evidence par bara conclusion nikalta hai ya complex issue ko bohat simple bana deta hai.

Iski 4 types hain:

#### 1. Hasty Generalization

**Definition:** Drawing a general conclusion from insufficient evidence.

**Roman Urdu:** Bohat kam examples dekh kar sab ke bare mein faisla kar lena.

Example: Do students maths mein fail hue, is liye tamam students maths mein weak hain.

#### 2. False Cause

**Definition:** Assuming that one event caused another without proper evidence.

**Roman Urdu:** Baghair saboot ke samajhna ke ek event doosre event ki wajah bana hai.

Example: Main ne baal dhoye aur us din barish ho gayi. Is liye main samajhta hoon ke mere baal dhone se barish hui.

#### 3. False Comparison

**Definition:** Comparing two things that are not sufficiently alike.

**Roman Urdu:** Do aisi cheezon ka muqabla karna jo asal mein ek jaisi nahi hotin.

Example: Gaon mein ghar ka darwaza lock nahi karte, is liye shehar mein bhi lock ki zaroorat nahi.

#### 4. Either-Or Fallacy

**Definition:** Presenting only two choices when more options actually exist.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab kisi maslay mein sirf do options dikhaye jayen halanke aur bhi options mojood hon.

Example: Ya tum mere saath ho ya mere khilaf.

### 8. Most Important Exam Points

1. Tone = Writer's attitude.
2. Irony has two types: Situational and Verbal.
3. Situational irony = Unexpected situation.
4. Verbal irony = Saying one thing but meaning the opposite.
5. Argument = Point + Supporting Evidence.
6. Fallacy = Error in reasoning.
7. Fallacies that ignore the issue = 3 types.
8. Fallacies that overgeneralize = 4 types.
9. Changing the Subject = Diverting attention.
10. Circular Reasoning = Same reason and conclusion.
11. Personal Attack = Attacking a person.
12. Hasty Generalization = Insufficient evidence.
13. False Cause = Wrong cause and effect.
14. False Comparison = Incorrect comparison.
15. Either-Or Fallacy = Limiting choices to two.
