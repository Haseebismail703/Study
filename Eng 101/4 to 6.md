# ENG101 — Lesson 5 & 6 Important Notes

## Lesson 5 — Connectives and Link Words: References

### 1. References

**Definition:** References are words that refer to other words or ideas in a text.

Roman Urdu: Reference words kisi pehle ya baad mein aane wale word ya idea ki taraf ishara karte hain.

**Important Reference Words:**

| Reference Word | Refers to |
|---|---|
| He | Male person |
| She | Female person |
| It | Thing / Animal |
| They | Plural persons or things |
| His | Belonging to a male |
| Their | Belonging to them |
| This / These | Previously mentioned idea or things |
| Who | Person |
| Which | Thing or idea |

**Examples:**

- Ali bought a car. **He** likes **it**.
  - He = Ali
  - It = car

- Sara and Ayesha are friends. **They** study together.
  - They = Sara and Ayesha

### 2. Connectives

**Definition:** Connectives are words or phrases that connect ideas and sentences.

Roman Urdu: Connectives sentences ke darmiyan relationship show karte hain, jaise contrast, result aur addition.

### 3. Types of Connectives / Link Words

**1. Result**

Result ya outcome show karte hain.

- Thus
- Therefore
- Hence
- Consequently
- As a consequence
- Accordingly

Example: He worked hard; therefore, he passed.

**2. Contrast**

Opposite ideas show karte hain.

- But
- However
- Although
- Nevertheless
- Instead
- On the other hand
- In contrast
- Yet

Example: He is poor, but he is honest.

**3. Comparison**

Similarities show karte hain.

- Similarly
- Likewise
- Like
- Equally
- In the same way
- Just as

Example: Ali likes cricket. Similarly, Ahmed likes cricket.

**4. Illustration / Example**

Examples dene ke liye use hote hain.

- For example
- For instance
- Such as
- To illustrate
- Including

Example: I like fruits, such as apples and bananas.

**5. Cause and Effect**

Reason aur uska result show karte hain.

- Because
- Because of
- Since
- Therefore
- As a result
- Consequently
- So

Example: He was ill, so he stayed home.

**6. A Series / Time Sequence**

Order ya time show karte hain.

- First
- Second
- Next
- Before
- After
- Then
- Finally
- Lastly
- Previously
- Immediately

Example: First, open the book. Then, read the lesson.

**7. Addition**

Extra information add karte hain.

- And
- Also
- Besides
- Furthermore
- In addition
- Moreover
- Another

Example: He is intelligent. Moreover, he is hardworking.

### Quick Revision — Lesson 5

- References = Words that refer to other words or ideas.
- Connectives = Words that connect ideas.
- However = Contrast.
- Therefore = Result.
- Similarly = Comparison.
- For example = Illustration.
- Because = Cause.
- Finally = Time sequence.
- Furthermore = Addition.

## Lesson 6 — Reading Skills

### 1. Reading Skills

This lesson focuses on understanding a passage, finding its main idea, locating specific information, and understanding words.

Roman Urdu: Passage ko samajhna, us mein se specific information dhoondhna aur words ke similar ya opposite meanings identify karna.

### 2. History of Computers

**Important points from the handout:**

| Person / Device | Important Information |
|---|---|
| Fingers | First calculating method |
| Abacus | Bead-based calculating device |
| J. Napier | Developed a mechanical method of multiplication and division |
| Henry Briggs | Produced logarithm tables |
| Newton and Leibnitz | Independently invented Calculus |
| Charles Babbage | Designed the Analytical Engine in 1830 |
| Vannevar Bush | Built the first analog computer in 1930 |
| Mark I | Completed in 1944 |
| Howard Aiken | Associated with Mark I |
| ENIAC | Built in 1946 using vacuum tubes |
| John von Neumann | Developed the idea of storing instructions in computer memory in 1947 |

### 3. Generations of Computers

**First Generation (1950)**

- Used vacuum tubes.
- Example: UNIVAC I.

**Second Generation (1960)**

- Used transistors instead of vacuum tubes.
- Smaller, faster and more dependable.

**Third Generation (1965)**

- Used integrated circuits.
- Could perform about one million calculations per second.

**Fourth Generation**

- Used smaller integrated circuits on a single chip.
- This reduction in size is called microminiaturization.

### 4. Locating Information

**Definition:** Locating information means finding specific information in a passage.

Roman Urdu: Passage mein kisi particular fact, date, person ya event ko dhoondhna.

Example questions:

- When was ENIAC built?
- Who designed the Analytical Engine?
- When did second-generation computers develop?

Tip: Question ke important keyword ko passage mein search karo.

### 5. Synonyms

**Definition:** Synonyms are words that have similar meanings.

Roman Urdu: Aise words jin ka meaning same ya milta julta ho.

| Word | Synonym |
|---|---|
| Machine | Device |
| Designed | Devised |
| A lot | A great deal |
| Errors | Mistakes |
| Solve | Figure out |

### 6. Antonyms

**Definition:** Antonyms are words that have opposite meanings.

Roman Urdu: Aise words jin ke meanings ek doosre ke opposite hon.

| Word | Antonym |
|---|---|
| Old | Modern |
| A few | Several |
| To include | Cut out |
| Contemporaries | Predecessors |
| Still in use | Obsolete |
