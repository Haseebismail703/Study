# ENG101 – Lesson 1

## Reading and Dictionary

### 1. Definition of Dictionary
A dictionary is a book that provides the meanings, spellings, pronunciation, and other information about words.

### 2. Uses of a Dictionary
1\. Spelling: It tells us how to spell a word correctly.

2\. Pronunciation: It tells us how to pronounce a word correctly.

3\. Part of Speech: It tells us whether a word is a noun, verb, adjective, etc.

4\. Origin of Word: It tells us where a word comes from.

5\. Meaning / Explanation: It explains the meaning of a word.

6\. Usage / Restrictive Labels: It tells us how and where a word is used.

7\. Synonyms and Antonyms: Synonyms have similar meanings, while antonyms have opposite meanings.

8\. Foreign Words and Phrases: It explains words and phrases from other languages.

9\. General Information: It provides additional information about words.

Revision Tip: Learn the definition of a dictionary and all 9 uses for your ENG101 exam.


How to Use a Dictionary
Meaning: Learning how to use a dictionary saves time.

Step 1: Alphabetical Order
Definition: All words in a dictionary are arranged in alphabetical order (A–Z).
- Words are arranged according to the first letter.
- If the first letters are the same, compare the second letter.
- If the first two letters are the same, compare the third letter, and so on.
Example: Apple, Ball, Cat, Dog.

Step 2: Finding Words Quickly
- Thumb through: Quickly flip through the dictionary pages.
- Look at guide words: Check the guide words at the top of each page.
- Find the nearest guide word: When you find the page closest to your required word, look down that page to find your word.
