# MTH202 — Lesson 3: Laws of Logic
## Short Exam Revision Notes

### 1. Basic Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¬p | NOT p |
| p ∧ q | AND |
| p ∨ q | OR |
| p → q | If p, then q |
| p ↔ q | If and only if |
| T / F | True / False |

## 2. Laws of Logic

### 1. Commutative Law

**Definition:** Changing the order does not change the result.

**Roman Urdu:** Statements ki jagah badal sakte hain.

- p ∨ q ≡ q ∨ p
- p ∧ q ≡ q ∧ p

**Forward / Reverse:** Dono directions mein order change kar sakte ho.

**Other possibility:**
- (p ∨ q) ≡ (q ∨ p)
- (p ∧ q) ≡ (q ∧ p)

**Kab use karein?** Jab terms ka order badalne se doosra law apply karna easy ho.

### 2. Associative Law

**Definition:** Changing the grouping does not change the result.

**Roman Urdu:** Brackets ki grouping badal sakte hain, order nahi.

- (p ∨ q) ∨ r ≡ p ∨ (q ∨ r)
- (p ∧ q) ∧ r ≡ p ∧ (q ∧ r)

**Forward:** (p ∨ q) ∨ r → p ∨ (q ∨ r)

**Reverse:** p ∨ (q ∨ r) → (p ∨ q) ∨ r

**Other possibility:**
- (p ∧ q) ∧ r ↔ p ∧ (q ∧ r)

**Kab use karein?** Jab brackets ki grouping badal kar same operation wale terms ko paas lana ho.

**Note:** Associative Law mein ∧ ko ∨ se replace nahi kar sakte.

### 3. Distributive Law

**Definition:** Expanding brackets or factoring a common term.

**Roman Urdu:** Brackets khol sakte hain ya common term bahar nikal sakte hain.

**AND over OR:**

- p ∧ (q ∨ r) ≡ (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)

**Reverse / Factor:**

- (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r) ≡ p ∧ (q ∨ r)

**OR over AND:**

- p ∨ (q ∧ r) ≡ (p ∨ q) ∧ (p ∨ r)

**Reverse / Factor:**

- (p ∨ q) ∧ (p ∨ r) ≡ p ∨ (q ∧ r)

**Other possibility:**

- (p ∨ ¬q) ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p ∨ (¬q ∧ q)

**Kab use karein?** Jab common term ho, brackets kholne hon, ya expression ko factor karna ho.

**Exam tip:** Distributive Law dono directions mein valid hai. Jo direction expression ko simple kare, woh use karo.

### 4. Identity Law

**Definition:** Combining a proposition with its identity value leaves it unchanged.

**Roman Urdu:** Original expression same rehta hai.

- p ∨ F ≡ p
- p ∧ T ≡ p

**Reverse:**
- p ≡ p ∨ F
- p ≡ p ∧ T

**Other possibilities:**
- F ∨ (p ∧ q) ≡ p ∧ q
- T ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p ∨ q

**Kab use karein?** Jab OR ke saath F ya AND ke saath T ho.

### 5. Domination Law

**Definition:** True dominates OR, and False dominates AND.

**Roman Urdu:** Result directly T ya F ban jata hai.

- p ∨ T ≡ T
- p ∧ F ≡ F

**Reverse:**
- T ≡ p ∨ T
- F ≡ p ∧ F

**Other possibilities:**
- (p ∧ q) ∨ T ≡ T
- (p ∨ q) ∧ F ≡ F

**Kab use karein?** Jab OR expression mein T ya AND expression mein F ho.

### 6. Negation Law

**Definition:** A proposition combined with its negation gives a fixed result.

**Roman Urdu:** Statement aur opposite ka OR True, AND False deta hai.

- p ∨ ¬p ≡ T
- p ∧ ¬p ≡ F

**Reverse:**
- T ≡ p ∨ ¬p
- F ≡ p ∧ ¬p

**Other possibilities:**
- q ∨ ¬q ≡ T
- ¬q ∧ q ≡ F

**Kab use karein?** Jab same variable aur uska NOT saath hon.

### 7. Idempotent Law

**Definition:** Repeating the same proposition does not change it.

**Roman Urdu:** Same statement do baar ho to ek hi rehta hai.

- p ∨ p ≡ p
- p ∧ p ≡ p

**Reverse:**
- p ≡ p ∨ p
- p ≡ p ∧ p

**Other possibility:**
- (p ∨ p) ∧ q ≡ p ∧ q

**Kab use karein?** Jab same term repeat ho raha ho.

### 8. Double Negative Law

**Definition:** Two negations cancel each other.

**Roman Urdu:** Do NOT signs khatam ho jate hain.

- ¬(¬p) ≡ p

**Reverse:**
- p ≡ ¬(¬p)

**Other possibilities:**
- ¬¬q ≡ q
- ¬(¬(p ∧ q)) ≡ p ∧ q

**Kab use karein?** Jab kisi variable ya expression par double NOT ho.

### 9. De Morgan's Law

**Definition:** Negation changes AND to OR, or OR to AND.

**Roman Urdu:** NOT bracket mein jaye to AND/OR change hote hain aur har term par NOT lagta hai.

- ¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q
- ¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q

**Reverse:**
- ¬p ∨ ¬q ≡ ¬(p ∧ q)
- ¬p ∧ ¬q ≡ ¬(p ∨ q)

**Other possibilities:**
- ¬(¬p ∧ q) ≡ ¬¬p ∨ ¬q
- ¬(p ∧ q ∧ r) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q ∨ ¬r

**Kab use karein?** Jab NOT poore bracket ke bahar ho, ya negated terms ko bracket mein combine karna ho.

### 10. Absorption Law

**Definition:** A proposition absorbs an expression containing itself.

**Roman Urdu:** Extra expression remove ho sakta hai.

- p ∨ (p ∧ q) ≡ p
- p ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p

**Reverse:**
- p ≡ p ∨ (p ∧ q)
- p ≡ p ∧ (p ∨ q)

**Other possibilities:**
- p ∨ (p ∧ ¬q) ≡ p
- p ∧ (p ∨ ¬q) ≡ p

**Kab use karein?** Jab same p bahar aur bracket ke andar ho, aur operations absorption pattern banayen.

## 3. Conditional Statement / Implication

**Definition:** A conditional statement is an if-then statement.

**Roman Urdu:** Agar p hoga, to q hoga.

- p = Hypothesis (IF part)
- q = Conclusion (THEN part)

**Formula:**

p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q

**Reverse:**

¬p ∨ q ≡ p → q

**Other possibility:**

¬(p → q) ≡ p ∧ ¬q

### Truth Table

| p | q | p → q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | T |
| T | F | F |
| F | T | T |
| F | F | T |

**Important:** Implication sirf T → F par False hoti hai.

### Types of Conditional

| Type | Formula |
|---|---|
| Original | p → q |
| Converse | q → p |
| Inverse | ¬p → ¬q |
| Contrapositive | ¬q → ¬p |

**Note:** Original aur contrapositive equivalent hain. Converse aur inverse equivalent hain.

## 4. Biconditional

**Definition:** A biconditional means “if and only if.”

**Roman Urdu:** Dono statements ki truth values same hon to result True hota hai.

**Formula:**

p ↔ q ≡ (p → q) ∧ (q → p)

**Other equivalent form:**

p ↔ q ≡ (p ∧ q) ∨ (¬p ∧ ¬q)

**Kab use karein?** Jab ↔ ko AND aur implication ya truth-value expression mein convert karna ho.

## 5. Logical Equivalence

**Definition:** Two propositions are logically equivalent if their truth values are always the same.

**Roman Urdu:** Dono expressions har situation mein same result dein.

**Symbol:** ≡

**Important:** Equivalent laws ko forward aur reverse dono directions mein use kiya ja sakta hai.

## 6. Simplification — Solved Examples

### Example 1: Identity Law

p ∨ F ≡ **p**

### Example 2: Double Negative Law

¬(¬p) ≡ **p**

### Example 3: De Morgan's Law

¬(p ∧ q) ≡ **¬p ∨ ¬q**

### Example 4: Distributive Law — Expand

p ∧ (q ∨ r)

≡ (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)

### Example 5: Distributive Law — Reverse / Factor

(p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)

≡ p ∧ (q ∨ r)

### Example 6: Implication

p → q ≡ **¬p ∨ q**

### Example 7: Multiple Laws

Simplify:

p ∨ ¬(¬p ∧ q)

**Step 1 — De Morgan's Law**

≡ p ∨ (¬¬p ∨ ¬q)

**Step 2 — Double Negative Law**

≡ p ∨ (p ∨ ¬q)

**Step 3 — Associative Law**

≡ (p ∨ p) ∨ ¬q

**Step 4 — Idempotent Law**

≡ **p ∨ ¬q**

### Example 8: Multiple Laws

Simplify:

¬(¬p ∧ q) ∧ (p ∨ q)

**Step 1 — De Morgan's Law**

≡ (¬¬p ∨ ¬q) ∧ (p ∨ q)

**Step 2 — Double Negative Law**

≡ (p ∨ ¬q) ∧ (p ∨ q)

**Step 3 — Distributive Law**

≡ p ∨ (¬q ∧ q)

**Step 4 — Negation Law**

≡ p ∨ F

**Step 5 — Identity Law**

≡ **p**

## Last-Minute Revision

- Commutative = Order change.
- Associative = Grouping change.
- Distributive = Expand or factor.
- Identity = F with OR; T with AND.
- Domination = T with OR; F with AND.
- Negation = p with ¬p gives T or F.
- Idempotent = Repeated p becomes p.
- Double Negative = Two NOT signs cancel.
- De Morgan's = Switch AND/OR and negate each term.
- Absorption = Extra expression disappears.
- Implication = T → F is False.
- Biconditional = Same truth values give T.

**Exam tip:** Har law ka reverse bhi check karo. Grouping ke liye Associative, expand/factor ke liye Distributive, aur negation ya identity patterns ke liye unke respective laws use karo. Jo valid direction expression ko simple kare, woh choose karo.
