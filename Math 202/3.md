## Short Exam Revision Notes

### 1. Basic Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¬p | NOT p |
| p ∧ q | AND |
| p ∨ q | OR |
| p → q | If p, then q |
| p ↔ q | If and only if |
| T / F | True / False |

## 2. Laws of Logic

### 1. Commutative Law
**Definition:** Changing the order does not change the result.

**Roman Urdu:** Statements ki jagah badalne se result nahi badalta.

- p ∨ q ≡ q ∨ p
- p ∧ q ≡ q ∧ p

### 2. Associative Law
**Definition:** Changing the grouping does not change the result.

**Roman Urdu:** Brackets ki grouping badalne se result nahi badalta.

- (p ∨ q) ∨ r ≡ p ∨ (q ∨ r)
- (p ∧ q) ∧ r ≡ p ∧ (q ∧ r)

### 3. Distributive Law
**Definition:** One operation can be distributed over another operation.

**Roman Urdu:** Common term ko bracket ke bahar nikal sakte hain.

- (p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r) ≡ p ∧ (q ∨ r)
- (p ∨ q) ∧ (p ∨ r) ≡ p ∨ (q ∧ r)

### 4. Identity Law
**Definition:** Combining a proposition with its identity value leaves it unchanged.

**Roman Urdu:** p ki value change nahi hoti.

- p ∨ F ≡ p
- p ∧ T ≡ p

### 5. Domination Law
**Definition:** True dominates OR, and False dominates AND.

**Roman Urdu:** OR mein True aur AND mein False final result decide karte hain.

- p ∨ T ≡ T
- p ∧ F ≡ F

### 6. Negation Law
**Definition:** A proposition combined with its negation gives a fixed result.

**Roman Urdu:** Statement aur opposite ka OR True, AND False hota hai.

- p ∨ ¬p ≡ T
- p ∧ ¬p ≡ F

### 7. Idempotent Law
**Definition:** Repeating the same proposition does not change it.

**Roman Urdu:** Ek hi statement do baar ho to ek hi reh jata hai.

- p ∨ p ≡ p
- p ∧ p ≡ p

### 8. Double Negative Law
**Definition:** Two negations cancel each other.

**Roman Urdu:** Do NOT signs khatam ho jate hain.

- ¬(¬p) ≡ p

### 9. De Morgan's Law
**Definition:** Negation changes AND to OR, or OR to AND.

**Roman Urdu:** NOT bracket mein jaye to AND aur OR change hote hain.

- ¬(p ∧ q) ≡ ¬p ∨ ¬q
- ¬(p ∨ q) ≡ ¬p ∧ ¬q

### 10. Absorption Law
**Definition:** A proposition absorbs a larger expression containing itself.

**Roman Urdu:** p ke saath p wala extra part ho to expression p ban sakta hai.

- p ∨ (p ∧ q) ≡ p
- p ∧ (p ∨ q) ≡ p

## 3. Conditional Statement / Implication

**Definition:** A conditional statement is an if-then statement.

**Roman Urdu:** Agar p hoga, to q hoga.

- p = Hypothesis (IF part)
- q = Conclusion (THEN part)

**Formula:**

p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q

**Truth Table:**

| p | q | p → q |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | T |
| T | F | F |
| F | T | T |
| F | F | T |

**Important:** Implication sirf T → F par False hoti hai.

### Types of Conditional

| Type | Formula |
|---|---|
| Original | p → q |
| Converse | q → p |
| Inverse | ¬p → ¬q |
| Contrapositive | ¬q → ¬p |

**Negation of Conditional:**

¬(p → q) ≡ p ∧ ¬q

## 4. Biconditional

**Definition:** A biconditional means “if and only if.”

**Roman Urdu:** Dono statements ek doosre ke liye condition hoti hain.

**Formula:**

p ↔ q ≡ (p → q) ∧ (q → p)

**Rule:** p aur q ki truth values same hon to True hota hai.

## 5. Logical Equivalence

**Definition:** Two propositions are logically equivalent if their truth values are always the same.

**Roman Urdu:** Dono expressions har situation mein same truth value dein.

**Symbol:** ≡

## 6. Simplification — Solved Examples

### Example 1: Identity Law

p ∨ F ≡ **p**

### Example 2: Double Negative Law

¬(¬p) ≡ **p**

### Example 3: De Morgan's Law

¬(p ∧ q) ≡ **¬p ∨ ¬q**

### Example 4: Distributive Law

(p ∧ q) ∨ (p ∧ r)

≡ p ∧ (q ∨ r)

### Example 5: Implication

p → q ≡ **¬p ∨ q**

### Example 6: Multiple Laws

Simplify:

p ∨ ¬(¬p ∧ q)

**Step 1 — De Morgan's Law**

≡ p ∨ (¬¬p ∨ ¬q)

**Step 2 — Double Negative Law**

≡ p ∨ (p ∨ ¬q)

**Step 3 — Associative Law**

≡ (p ∨ p) ∨ ¬q

**Step 4 — Idempotent Law**

≡ **p ∨ ¬q**

## Last-Minute Revision

- Commutative = Order change.
- Associative = Grouping change.
- Distributive = Expand or factor.
- Identity = p remains unchanged.
- Domination = Result becomes T or F.
- Negation = p with NOT p.
- Idempotent = Repeated p becomes p.
- Double Negative = Two NOT signs cancel.
- De Morgan's = AND and OR switch.
- Absorption = Extra expression disappears.
- Implication = T → F is False.
- Biconditional = Both truth values must match.
