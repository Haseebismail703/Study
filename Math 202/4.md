# LAWS OF LOGIC

## 1. Commutative Law

**Formula:**

p ↔ q ≡ q ↔ p

**Roman Urdu Explanation:**

Is law mein hum p aur q ki position change kar sakte hain. Position change karne se answer change nahi hota.

**Trick:** Position change = Commutative Law.

## 2. Implication Law

**Formula:**

p → q ≡ ¬p ∨ q

**Roman Urdu Explanation:**

Is law mein implication arrow (→) ko remove karte hain, p ke upar NOT (¬) lagate hain aur OR (∨) use karte hain.

**Trick:** Arrow remove + NOT + OR.

## 3. Exportation Law

**Formula:**

(p ∧ q) → r ≡ p → (q → r)

**Roman Urdu Explanation:**

Is law mein p aur q ke darmiyan AND (∧) hota hai. Hum isko nested implication mein convert kar dete hain.

Yani q ko doosre implication ke andar le jate hain.

**Trick:** AND ko nested implication mein convert karna.

## 4. Equivalence Law

**Formula:**

p ↔ q ≡ (p → q) ∧ (q → p)

**Roman Urdu Explanation:**

Is law mein double arrow (↔) ko do single arrows (→) mein convert karte hain aur dono ke darmiyan AND (∧) lagate hain.

**Trick:** Double Arrow = Two Single Arrows + AND.

## 5. Reductio ad Absurdum Law

**Formula:**

p → q ≡ (p ∧ ¬q) → c

**Roman Urdu Explanation:**

Is law mein hum p ko TRUE aur q ko FALSE assume karte hain. Agar is combination se contradiction (c) milta hai, to hum apna logical proof establish kar sakte hain.

Yahan c ka matlab contradiction hai.

**Trick:** Contradiction ke zariye proof karna.

# QUICK REVISION

1. Commutative = Position change.
2. Implication = NOT + OR.
3. Exportation = AND to nested implication.
4. Equivalence = Two implications + AND.
5. Reductio ad Absurdum = Contradiction se proof.
