# MCM101 – Lecture 18
## Industrialization of Print Processes

### 1. Development of the Printing Press

**Block Printing (Xylography)**
- Original method of printing.
- Wooden blocks par individually text carve kiya jata tha.
- China mein originate hui.
- Earliest known printed text: **Diamond Sutra (868 A.D.)**
- Har page ke liye separate block banana parta tha, is liye process slow tha.

**Movable Type**
- Invented by **Bi Sheng in China (1041 A.D.)**
- Bi Sheng ne clay type use kiya, jo easily break ho jata tha.
- Korea ke Goryeo government ne 15th century ke early period mein metal type production support ki.
- Movable type mein individual characters ko arrange karke text print kiya jata hai.

**Johann Gutenberg**
- German goldsmith.
- Europe mein movable type printing ko widespread banaya.
- Printing press: around **1440**
- Separate metal characters aur oil-based ink use ki.
- Rag paper China se Muslims ke through Europe mein introduce hua.
- Alternative inventor claim: **Laurens Janszoon Coster (Dutch)**

### 2. Impact of Printing

- Books ki production aur availability increase hui.
- Handwritten books ke muqable printing fast aur cheaper thi.
- Knowledge aur information zyada logon tak pohanchi.
- Education aur literacy ko promote kiya.
- Printed books ne scientific knowledge aur religious ideas spread kiye.

**Incunabula:** Gutenberg ke first printed works se lekar **1500 A.D.** tak produced books.

### 3. Art of Book Printing and Typeface

- Pehle book printing ko art form samjha jata tha.
- Typesetting ka matlab page par letters/characters ko arrange karna hai.
- Germany mein typesetting ko “Black Art” kaha jata tha.
- Modern computer typesetting ne manual work ko kam kar diya.

### 4. Printing in the Industrial Age

**Steam-Powered Press**
- Inventors: **Friedrich Gottlob Koenig and Andreas Friedrich Bauer**
- Developed: **1812**
- 1814 mein unhon ne apni press *The Times* newspaper ko sell ki.
- Press tens of thousands copies per day print kar sakti thi.
- Later, press dono sides of paper par printing karne ke qabil hui.

**Rotary Press**
- Inventor: **Richard M. Hoe (United States)**
- Millions of copies per day print karna possible hua.
- Rolled paper / continuous feed se printing speed increase hui.

### 5. Lithography

- Developed by **Alois Senefelder (1798)**
- Lithography ka meaning: **Writing on stone**
- Principle: **Oil and water do not mix.**
- Printing mein image aur non-image areas oil/water ke difference se separate hote hain.

### 6. Offset Printing

- Inked image pehle plate se **rubber blanket** par transfer hoti hai.
- Phir rubber blanket se paper/printing surface par transfer hoti hai.

**Process:** Plate → Rubber Blanket → Paper

**Advantages**
- High-quality, sharp and clean printing.
- Different surfaces par print ho sakti hai.
- Printing plates easily prepare hoti hain.
- Plate ki life longer hoti hai.

**Important dates**
- First lithographic offset press: England, around **1875**
- Cardboard blanket later rubber se replace hui.
- Paper par offset printing: **Ira Washington Rubel (1903)** — likely credited.
- 1950s mein offset printing commercial printing ki most popular form bani.

### 7. Photo Offset

- Documents ko pehle film negatives par record kiya jata hai.
- Negative se image photomechanical printing plate par transfer hoti hai.
- Light aur chemical reaction ki madad se image plate par prepare hoti hai.

### 8. Present-Day Printing

- Offset printing high-volume commercial printing mein commonly used hai.
- Newspapers aur large quantity printing mein offset important hai.
- Digital inkjet presses bhi modern printing mein use hoti hain.

### 9. Desktop Publishing (DTP)

**DTP:** Computer par page layout aur publication design karne ka process.

Examples of software:
- PageMaker
- QuarkXPress

**Features**
- Page design and layout
- Magazine-style columns
- Page and chapter numbering
- Text and graphics alignment
- Text ko images ke around arrange karna

DTP 1980s mein personal computers ke popular hone ke sath widely known hua.
