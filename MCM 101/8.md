# MCM101 — Lecture 8: Message – Root of Communication II

## 1. Message Is the Most Sensitive Area

**Definition:** A message is the most sensitive part of communication because it can create confusion and conflict.

**Example:** Two people hear the same statement but understand it differently.

## 2. Problems in Understanding a Message

A sender cannot be 100% sure that the receiver will understand the message in the same way.

**Example:** A person makes a joke, but the other person takes it seriously.

## 3. Mass Communication and Culture

People from different cultures may understand the same message differently because their social and cultural values are different.

**Example:** A joke that is acceptable in one culture may offend people in another culture.

## 4. Cartoons in Communication

Newspaper cartoons can combine verbal and non-verbal communication. They use words, pictures, symbols, and facial expressions to convey a message.

**Example:** A political cartoon uses a picture and a few words to criticize a politician.

## 5. Propaganda

**Definition:** Propaganda is the use of messages to influence people's opinions or support a particular purpose.

**Example:** A group spreads one-sided information to make people support its ideas.

## 6. Commercial Message

**Definition:** A commercial message is used to promote a product or service and gain financial benefit.

**Example:** An advertisement claims that a product is the best to attract customers.

## 7. Solutions to Message Problems

### 7.1 Use of Standard Language

**Definition:** Standard language means using polite and decent words that people can easily understand and accept.

**Example:** Use respectful words instead of harsh or insulting language.

### 7.2 Brevity of Message

**Definition:** Brevity means keeping a message short and clear.

**Example:** Give a short and clear statement instead of a long, confusing explanation.

### 7.3 Ethics

**Definition:** Ethics means following moral values and respecting the social and religious values of others while preparing a message.

**Example:** Avoid creating a message that unnecessarily hurts people's cultural or religious feelings.
