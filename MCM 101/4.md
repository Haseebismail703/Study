# MCM101 — Lesson 4
## ELEMENTS OF COMMUNICATION AND EARLY COMMUNICATION MODELS

### 1. Elements of Communication

**Definition:** Communication is a process of sending and receiving messages.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab ek person doosre person ko information, idea ya message deta hai, to isay communication kehte hain.

**Example:** Teacher class mein lecture deta hai aur students usay sunte hain.

Communication different elements se mil kar banti hai, jo message ko sender se receiver tak pohanchane mein help karte hain.

### 2. Sender

**Definition:** The sender is the person who starts communication by sending a message.

**Roman Urdu:** Sender woh person hota hai jo communication start karta hai aur message bhejta hai.

**Example:** Tum apne dost ko WhatsApp message bhejte ho. Tum sender ho.

**Important Quiz Point:** Sender is the chief initiator of communication.

### 3. Message

**Definition:** A message is the information or idea sent by the sender.

**Roman Urdu:** Sender jo information, baat ya idea doosre person ko bhejta hai, woh message hota hai.

**Example:** Tum dost ko "Kal class hai" likh kar bhejte ho. Ye message hai.

**Important Quiz Point:** Message is the content being communicated.

### 4. Channel

**Definition:** A channel is the medium used to send a message.

**Roman Urdu:** Channel woh zariya hota hai jis ke through message receiver tak pohanchta hai.

**Examples:**
- Face-to-face communication: Voice or sound.
- WhatsApp: Mobile and internet.
- Television: Broadcast signal.

**Important Quiz Point:** Channel means medium of communication.

### 5. Receiver

**Definition:** The receiver is the person for whom the message is intended.

**Roman Urdu:** Receiver woh person hota hai jiske liye message bheja jata hai.

**Example:** Tum apne dost ko message bhejte ho. Tum sender aur tumhara dost receiver hai.

**Important Quiz Point:** The receiver is the intended destination of a message.

### 6. The Interpreter

**Definition:** An interpreter helps understand the meaning of a message.

**Roman Urdu:** Message ko samajhne ke liye person apni language, knowledge aur experience use karta hai. Agar message samajh na aaye, to communication mein problem ho sakti hai.

**Example:** Teacher English mein lecture deta hai aur student uska meaning samajh leta hai.

### 7. Noise

**Definition:** Noise is anything that disturbs or affects communication.

**Roman Urdu:** Noise woh cheez hai jo message ko sahi tarah receive ya understand karne mein problem paida kare.

**Types and Examples:**

- **Physical Noise:** Class mein shor ki wajah se teacher ki awaaz samajh na aana.
- **Semantic Noise:** Mushkil words ya language ki wajah se message ka meaning samajh na aana.

**Important Quiz Point:** Physical noise aur semantic noise ka difference yaad rakho.

### 8. The Decoder

**Definition:** The decoder changes a received signal into a form that can be understood.

**Roman Urdu:** Decoder signal ko decode karta hai, taake message samajhne layak ban sake.

**Example:** Receiver ko coded signal milta hai aur decoder usay understandable form mein change karta hai.

**Important Quiz Point:** Decoder aur receiver ko confuse mat karna. Shannon-Weaver model mein decoder signal ko decode karta hai.

---
### 10. Feedback

**Easy English Definition:** Feedback is the response given by the receiver to the sender's message.

**Roman Urdu Explanation:** Jab sender kisi ko message bhejta hai aur receiver uska jawab deta hai, to us jawab ko feedback kehte hain.

**Example:** Teacher asks, "Did you understand the lesson?" Student replies, "Yes, I understood." The student's reply is feedback.

**Important Quiz Points:**
- Feedback is the receiver's response to a message.
- Feedback helps the sender know whether the message was understood.
- Feedback helps make communication more effective.

### 11. Context

**Easy English Definition:** Context is the situation or background in which communication takes place.

**Roman Urdu Explanation:** Context ka matlab woh situation, jagah, waqt aur background hai jahan communication ho rahi hoti hai. Context message ka meaning samajhne mein help karta hai.

**Example:** In a classroom, a teacher says, "Please keep quiet." The classroom situation helps students understand the message.

**Important Quiz Points:**
- Context means the situation or background of communication.
- Context helps people understand the meaning of a message.
- The same message can have different meanings in different situations.

### Difference Between Feedback and Context

| Feedback | Context |
|---|---|
| Response to a message | Situation or background of communication |
| Receiver ka jawab | Communication ki situation |
| Example: "I understood." | Example: Classroom or hospital |

### Roman Urdu Quick Revision

- **Feedback:** Receiver ka sender ke message par jawab.
- **Context:** Woh situation ya background jahan communication hoti hai.


# MCM101 — Communication Model
## Short Revision Notes

### 1. Communication Model

**Definition:** A communication model shows the process of communication in a simple diagram.

**Roman Urdu:** Communication model diagram ki form mein dikhata hai ke message kaise sender se receiver tak pohanchta hai.

**Example:** Teacher → Message → Student

### 2. What is a Model?

**Definition:** A model is a simple representation of an object, event, or process.

**Roman Urdu:** Model kisi cheez ya process ko simple form mein represent karta hai.

**Important Points:**
- **Systematic Representation:** A model presents something in an organized way.
- **Communication Models are Pictures:** They show the communication process in a simple picture.
- **Models are Metaphors:** They help us understand one thing through another.

### 3. The Shannon-Weaver's Model of Communication

**Definition:** The Shannon-Weaver model explains how a message travels from a source to a receiver.

**Important Names:** Claude Shannon and Warren Weaver

**Year:** 1948 is commonly associated with the published model; Shannon's original paper was published in 1948.

### Six Elements of the Model

| Element | Simple English Definition |
|---|---|
| 1. Source | The person or place from which a message originates. |
| 2. Encoder | Converts information into a signal for transmission. |
| 3. Message | The information being communicated. |
| 4. Channel | The medium through which the message travels. |
| 5. Decoder | Converts the received signal into an understandable form. |
| 6. Receiver | The person or destination that receives the message. |

### Example

A teacher sends a message to a student.

- **Source:** Teacher
- **Encoder:** System that converts the message into a signal
- **Message:** "Submit your assignment tomorrow."
- **Channel:** Internet or another communication medium
- **Decoder:** System that converts the received signal into readable text
- **Receiver:** Student

### Important Difference: Receiver and Interpreter

- **Receiver:** The person who receives the message.
- **Interpreting:** Understanding the meaning of the message.
- **Interpreter:** In general communication, a person who understands or explains a message; in language communication, an interpreter may translate between languages.

**Definition:** An interpreter is a person who understands the meaning of a message.

### Context

**Definition:** Context is the situation in which communication takes place.

**Roman Urdu:** Context woh situation ya mahaul hai jisme communication hoti hai.

**Example:** A teacher giving a lecture in a classroom.

### Quick Revision

- A communication model shows the communication process.
- The Shannon-Weaver model explains message transmission.
- Source starts the message.
- Encoder converts information into a signal.
- Message is the information being communicated.
- Channel carries the message.
- Decoder processes the received signal.
- Receiver receives the message.
- Context is the situation in which communication takes place.
- Interpreting means understanding the meaning of a message.
