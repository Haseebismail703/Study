# MCM101 — Lesson 6: Types and Forms of Communication

## 1. Types of Communication

There are two main types of communication:

1. Intrapersonal Communication
2. Interpersonal Communication

### 1.1 Intrapersonal Communication

**Definition:** Communication that takes place within one individual.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab insan apne aap se communicate karta hai, jaise sochna ya apne dil mein baat karna.

**Examples:**
- Thinking
- Meditation
- Monologue (self-talk)
- Dreaming while asleep

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Within one individual
- Communication with oneself
- Sender and receiver are the same person

### 1.2 Interpersonal Communication

**Definition:** Communication involving two or more individuals exchanging information.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab do ya do se zyada log information share karte hain.

**Example:** Two friends talking to each other.

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Two or more individuals
- Exchange of information
- Direct interaction

**Interpersonal Communication has four types:**

1. Direct Interpersonal Communication
2. Group Communication
3. Organizational Communication
4. Mass Communication

### 1.2.1 Direct Interpersonal Communication

**Definition:** Communication in which two or more individuals communicate directly with each other.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab do ya zyada log directly aik doosray se baat karte hain.

**Example:** Two friends talking face-to-face.

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Direct communication
- Face-to-face interaction
- Two or more individuals

### 1.2.2 Group Communication

**Definition:** Communication in which people exchange views while enjoying equal status on one count or another.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab group ke log apni opinions aur ideas share karte hain.

**Examples:**
- Classmates
- Hockey team players
- Doctors discussing a topic
- Teachers sharing views

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Group members
- Exchange of views
- Equal status
- Shared interests or category

### 1.2.3 Organizational Communication

**Definition:** Communication that takes place through the formal structure and vertical lines of an organization.

**Roman Urdu:** Organization mein information ya instructions aik hierarchy ke through pass hoti hain.

**Example:**

Director → Manager → Field Officer → Other Staff

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Vertical lines
- Chain of command
- Formal structure
- Rules and hierarchy
- Army, company, NGO, educational institution

**MCQ Trick:** Director ya manager ke through instructions pass hon to Organizational Communication yaad karo.

### 1.2.4 Mass Communication

**Definition:** Communication from one source intended for a very large and diverse audience.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab aik source bohat saare mukhtalif logon tak information pohanchata hai, aam tor par technology ke zariye.

**Examples:**
- Television broadcast
- Radio programme
- Newspaper
- Online news

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- One source
- Large audience
- Diverse audience
- Technology and mass media
- Distance, nationality, religion and other differences

**MCQ Trick:** One source + large audience + technology = Mass Communication.

## 2. Forms of Communication

There are three forms of communication discussed in this lesson:

1. Verbal Communication
2. Non-verbal Communication
3. Combination of Verbal and Non-verbal Communication

### 2.1 Verbal Communication

**Definition:** Communication through spoken or written words.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab words bol kar ya likh kar message convey kiya jaye.

**Examples:**
- Speaking face-to-face
- Writing a letter
- Books and newspapers
- Text messages
- Radio commentary

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Words
- Speaking
- Writing
- Oral communication
- Written communication
- Language

**MCQ Trick:** Spoken or written words = Verbal Communication.

### 2.2 Non-verbal Communication

**Definition:** Communication through gestures, signs, symbols, facial expressions and other non-word signals.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab message convey karne ke liye words ke bajaye gestures, expressions ya signs use hon.

**Examples:**
- Facial expressions
- Hand gestures
- Traffic signals
- Symbols and signs
- Body language

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Gestures
- Facial expressions
- Body language
- Signs and symbols
- Human senses

**Important Lecture Figure:** Video mein approximately **90% non-verbal** aur **10% verbal** communication ka estimate diya gaya hai. Is figure ko lecture ke MCQs ke liye yaad rakho; yeh universal scientific rule nahi hai.

**MCQ Trick:** Gesture, sign, symbol or facial expression = Non-verbal Communication.

### 2.3 Combination of Verbal and Non-verbal Communication

**Definition:** Communication that uses both words and non-verbal signals.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab message mein words ke saath tone, facial expressions, images ya gestures bhi use hon.

**Examples:**
- TV presenter speaking with facial expressions and graphics
- Teacher explaining with words and hand gestures
- News report containing text, images and spoken commentary

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Both verbal and non-verbal
- Words plus gestures
- Tone and expressions
- Images and graphics

**MCQ Trick:** Words + gestures/images/expressions = Combination.

## 3. Important Differences for MCQs

| Question clue | Correct answer |
|---|---|
| Thinking or dreaming | Intrapersonal |
| Two or more people exchanging information | Interpersonal |
| Face-to-face direct interaction | Direct Interpersonal |
| Classmates or hockey team sharing views | Group |
| Director, manager and chain of command | Organizational |
| One source reaching a large audience | Mass |
| Spoken or written words | Verbal |
| Gestures, signs or facial expressions | Non-verbal |
| Words plus gestures or graphics | Combination |
| Communication through vertical lines | Organizational |
| Technology used to reach a vast audience | Mass |

## 4. Quick Revision: Important Numbers and Keywords

- **2 main types:** Intrapersonal and Interpersonal.
- **4 types of Interpersonal Communication:** Direct, Group, Organizational and Mass.
- **3 forms:** Verbal, Non-verbal and Combination.
- **90% / 10%:** Approximate non-verbal/verbal figures stated in the lecture.
- **One individual:** Intrapersonal.
- **Two or more individuals:** Interpersonal.
- **Equal status and exchange of views:** Group communication.
- **Vertical lines and chain of command:** Organizational communication.
- **One source and large diverse audience:** Mass communication.
- **Spoken or written words:** Verbal communication.
- **Gestures, signs and symbols:** Non-verbal communication.
- **Words plus non-verbal signals:** Combination.

## 5. Most Important MCQ Strategy

When reading an MCQ, look for its identifying keyword:

1. Thinking, meditation, dreaming → Intrapersonal.
2. Two or more individuals exchanging information → Interpersonal.
3. Direct face-to-face conversation → Direct Interpersonal.
4. Classmates, team players, equal status → Group.
5. Director, manager, hierarchy, vertical lines → Organizational.
6. One source, large audience, mass media → Mass.
7. Spoken words or written words → Verbal.
8. Gestures, body language, signs → Non-verbal.
9. Words with images, tone or gestures → Combination.

**Remember:** Group and Organizational Communication both involve multiple people, but Group Communication emphasizes exchanging views and equal status, while Organizational Communication emphasizes formal hierarchy and chain of command.
