# MCM101 – Lecture 13
## Mass Media – Historical Perspective

### 1. Introduction to Mass Media History

In early times, people wanted to share information and knowledge with others.

But there was no proper mass communication system.

- People shared information through travellers.
- War expeditions also brought information from distant places.
- Travelling was difficult because maps and route knowledge were limited.
- Language helped people communicate with a small number of people.
- Paper was an important step, but it did not create mass communication by itself.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** In early times, information from distant places was mainly shared through **Travellers and war expeditions**.

### 2. Books – First Fascination Towards Mass Media

Books were one of the earliest forms of mass media.

The exact date and name of the first book are unknown.

According to the handout, people had some idea about books in the **7th century**.

Books helped people share knowledge with many others, regardless of:
- Cast
- Colour
- Religion
- Rich or poor status

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Books became an early form of mass media in the **7th century**.

### 3. Four Early Periods in the History of Books

Remember these four periods carefully.

**1. 7th to 13th Century – Religious Manuscripts**

- Books were written by hand.
- Most books were religious.
- Creating books was considered an act of worship.

**2. 13th to 15th Century – Secularization of Book Production**

- Books started covering non-religious topics.
- They explained the world around people.
- Books were still written by hand.

Two main reasons:
- Rise of universities in Europe.
- Crusaders brought old texts from Byzantium.

**3. 15th to 16th Century – First Printed Books**

- Printed versions of traditional books appeared.
- Bible, prayer books and religious calendars were common.

**4. 16th to 17th Century – New Information**

- New information was added to books.
- These books had important effects on European society.

**⭐ Important MCQ:**

| Period | Main concept |
|---|---|
| 7th–13th Century | Religious manuscripts |
| 13th–15th Century | Secular books |
| 15th–16th Century | First printed books |
| 16th–17th Century | New information in books |

### 4. Illuminated Manuscripts

**Easy English Definition:** Illuminated manuscripts are handwritten books decorated with beautiful designs and pictures.

Roman Urdu: Purane zamane mein monks books ko haath se likhte aur un par khoobsurat designs aur pictures banate thay.

Important points:
- They were mainly produced in monasteries.
- Most were religious books.
- Monks made copies of books.
- Their decoration was very detailed.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Illuminated manuscripts were mainly produced in **Monasteries**.

### 5. Book of Kells

The Book of Kells is an example of an illuminated manuscript.

Important points:
- It was produced between the 7th and 9th centuries.
- It is famous for its beautiful decoration.
- It contains religious content.
- It is associated with the life and message of Christ.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Book of Kells is an example of **Illuminated Manuscript**.

### 6. Book of Durrow

Important points:
- It was created around 680 A.D.
- It contains decorative patterns.
- Its designs include interlace.

**Interlace:** A design made by weaving lines or shapes together.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Book of Durrow was created around **680 A.D.**

### 7. Early Analysis of Manuscripts

Old handwritten books had some problems:

1. They were very expensive.
2. They took a long time to produce.
3. They were not easily available to the public.
4. Many books were kept in monasteries.
5. There was no proper library catalog system.

**Roman Urdu:** Books banana mushkil aur mehnga tha. Is liye aam logon ke liye books hasil karna asaan nahi tha.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Manuscript books were difficult to access because they were kept in **Monasteries**.

### 8. Rise of Print-Dominated Society

Between the 13th and 16th centuries, society started moving towards printed information.

Important changes:
- People received more information.
- Scientific books became popular.
- Religious education increased.
- Books were used for propaganda.
- The Church's control over information started decreasing.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** The rise of a print-dominated society took place between the **13th and 16th centuries**.

### 9. Ancient History of Printing

The earliest printing method was **Block Printing**.

**Easy English Definition:** Block printing is a method in which carved wooden blocks are used to print text or images on paper.

Important points:
- Block printing originated in China.
- A separate wooden block was made for each page.
- It was a slow process.
- It was difficult to print different books.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** The original method of printing was **Block Printing**.

### 10. Diamond Sutra

Important points:
- It is a Buddhist scripture.
- It was printed in China.
- It dates back to 868 A.D.
- It is the earliest known printed text mentioned in the handout.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Diamond Sutra was printed in **868 A.D.**

### 11. Movable Type Printing

**Easy English Definition:** Movable type means separate letters or characters that can be arranged to make different pages.

Important points:
- Movable type was invented by Bi Sheng in China.
- It was invented in 1041 A.D.
- He used clay type.
- Clay type could break easily.
- Korea later developed metal type.

**⭐ Important MCQs:**
- Inventor of movable type: **Bi Sheng**
- Country: **China**
- Year: **1041 A.D.**
- Original material used by Bi Sheng: **Clay**

### 12. From China to Germany – Gutenberg

Johann Gutenberg was a German goldsmith.

Important points:
- He improved the printing process in Europe.
- He used movable type widely.
- He is associated with the printing press of the 1440s.
- He used oil-based ink.
- He worked as a goldsmith before inventing the printing press.

**⭐ Important MCQs:**
- Gutenberg belonged to: **Germany**
- His profession: **Goldsmith**
- Printing press: **1440s**
- Important contribution: **Widespread use of movable type**

### 13. Laurens Janszoon Coster

The handout also mentions Laurens Janszoon Coster.

Important points:
- He was Dutch.
- He is also credited by some people with the invention of the printing press.
- The claim about Gutenberg is not accepted by everyone.

**⭐ Important MCQ:** Another person credited with the printing press was **Laurens Janszoon Coster**.
