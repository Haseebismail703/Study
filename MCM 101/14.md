## Introduction (Lecture Overview)

In this lecture, we will study how print media started and developed around the world. We will learn about the history of newspapers, magazines, pamphlets, printing technology and the development of journalism in the Subcontinent.

## 14.1 Emergence of Print Media

**Definition:** Print media is a form of mass communication that uses printed materials to share information with people.

**Roman Urdu:** Print media ka matlab printed cheezon ke zariye logon tak information pohanchana hai.

Examples:
- Newspapers
- Magazines
- Books
- Newsletters
- Pamphlets

Printing technology made it possible to produce multiple copies of information and distribute them among people.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing ki wajah se ek hi information ki bohat sari copies bana kar zyada logon tak pohanchana mumkin hua.

## 14.3 Print Comes to South Asia

For almost 100 years, people in the Subcontinent remained unaware of printing technology.

Newspapers and magazines were brought by ships from the UK, mainly for English people living in the Subcontinent.

Print media developed in the Subcontinent during British rule.

**Roman Urdu:** South Asia mein printing media British rulers ke zariye introduce hui. Shuru mein UK se newspapers aur magazines ships ke zariye aate thay.

## 14.4 Colonial Journalism

**Definition:** Colonial journalism refers to journalism that developed during the period of colonial rule.

**Roman Urdu:** British hukoomat ke daur mein Subcontinent mein jo journalism develop hui usay colonial journalism kehte hain.

**William Bolts (1776)**

- William Bolts attempted to start the first newspaper in India in 1776.
- He could not succeed because of opposition from the East India Company.

**Roman Urdu:** William Bolts ne India mein newspaper shuru karne ki koshish ki lekin East India Company ki opposition ki wajah se kamyab nahi hua.

## 14.5 Bengal

**Hickey's Bengal Gazette (1780)**

- Started by James Augustus Hickey in 1780.
- Considered the first regular publication from Indian soil.
- It was a two-sheet newspaper.
- It wrote about the private lives of British officials.
- Hickey criticized Governor-General Warren Hastings and his wife.
- Hickey faced imprisonment and fines.
- He received four months imprisonment and Rs. 500 fine.
- Later, he received one year imprisonment and Rs. 5,000 fine.

**Important Names:**
- James Augustus Hickey
- Warren Hastings

**Roman Urdu:** Hickey ne British officials par tanqeed ki jis ki wajah se usay jail aur jurmane ka samna karna para.

## 14.7 Madras (Chennai)

Important events and publications:

**1785 – Madras Courier**
- Started by Richard Johnson.
- Became an important newspaper in Madras.
- Became an official information publication.

**1791 – Hurkaru**
- Started by Hugh Boyd.
- Publication stopped after Boyd's death.

**1795 – Madras Gazette and India Herald**
- Both were important early publications in Madras.
- Humphreys, founder of India Herald, was deported.

**1878 – The Hindu**
- Founded in 1878.
- Played an important role in promoting Indian independence.
- Founder: Kasturi Ranga Iyengar.

**Important Names:**
- Richard Johnson
- Hugh Boyd
- Humphreys
- Kasturi Ranga Iyengar

## 14.8 Bombay (Mumbai)

Important events:

- 1789 – Bombay Herald was started.
- 1790 – Bombay Courier started carrying advertisements in Gujarati.
- 1791 – Bombay Gazette was started.
- 1792 – Bombay Gazette merged with Bombay Herald.

The merged publication carried official notifications and advertisements.

**Roman Urdu:** Bombay mein newspapers ke saath advertisements ka trend bhi develop hua. Newspapers official information aur advertisements publish karte thay.

## Important Keywords

- **Print Media:** Printed material used to share information.
- **Pamphlet:** A small printed booklet or paper.
- **Colonial Journalism:** Journalism during colonial rule.
- **Circulation:** Number of copies distributed.
- **Penny Press:** Cheap newspapers that helped mass communication.
- **Yellow Journalism:** Sensational style of journalism.
- **Associated Press:** News agency established in 1848.
- **Specialization:** Development of different services in print media.
- **Press Freedom:** Freedom to publish news and opinions without unfair government restrictions.
