# MCM101 – Lesson 5
## Communication Models – Graphic Presentation of Complex Issues

### 1. Communication Model
A communication model is a simplified representation of the communication process. It helps us understand how communication takes place.

Roman Urdu: Communication model ek simple diagram ya representation hota hai jo samjhata hai ke communication kis tarah hoti hai.

### 2. Shannon-Weaver Model
The Shannon-Weaver Model explains how a message is transmitted from a sender to a receiver through a channel.

Main elements:
- Information Source: Message banane wala.
- Encoder/Transmitter: Message ko signal mein convert karta hai.
- Channel: Message bhejne ka zariya.
- Noise: Koi bhi disturbance jo message ko affect kare.
- Decoder/Receiver: Signal ko samajhne ke liye decode karta hai.
- Destination: Jis tak message pohanchta hai.

Limitation:
This model gives more attention to the transmission of a message and less attention to how people interpret its meaning.

Roman Urdu: Is model mein message bhejne par zyada focus hai, lekin receiver message ka kya matlab samajhta hai, is par kam focus hai.

### 3. Advantages of Communication Models
1. General Perspective: Models humein communication ka overall view dete hain.
2. Clarify Complexity: Mushkil communication process ko samajhna asaan banate hain.
3. New Discoveries: Models naye ideas aur discoveries ki taraf rehnumai kar sakte hain.

### 4. Limitations of Communication Models
1. Over-simplification: Model communication ko zaroorat se zyada simple bana sakta hai.
2. Confusion Between Model and Reality: Hum model ko asal communication samajh sakte hain, jabke model sirf uski representation hota hai.
3. Premature Closure: Tamam relevant information par ghour kiye baghair jaldi conclusion nikal lena.

### 5. Schramm-Osgood Interactive Model
Wilbur Schramm ne Shannon-Weaver ke mathematical model ko modify kiya. Schramm-Osgood Interactive Model communication ko two-way process ke taur par explain karta hai.

Main features:
- Communication two-way hoti hai.
- Sender aur receiver dono encoding aur decoding kar sakte hain.
- Interpreter message ka meaning samajhne mein madad karta hai.
- Feedback communication ka important part hai.
- Sender aur receiver ke fields of experience message ko samajhne par asar dalte hain.
- Feedback foran bhi aa sakta hai aur delay se bhi.

### 6. Field of Experience
Field of experience se murad kisi shakhs ka knowledge, experiences, attitudes aur background hai, jo message ko samajhne par asar dalta hai.

Example: Agar teacher technical English mein samjhaye aur student ko English kam aati ho, to student message ko samajhne mein mushkil mehsoos kar sakta hai.

### 7. Feedback
Feedback receiver ka response hota hai jo sender ko batata hai ke message ka kya asar hua.

Types discussed in the lesson:

1. Direct Feedback: Jab receiver seedha jawab de.
2. Moderately Direct Feedback: Jab receiver body language ya facial expressions se reaction de.
3. Indirect Feedback: Jab receiver ka response seedha verbal jawab na ho aur uska asar doosre tareeqe se samne aaye.

Example:
Teacher sawal poochta hai aur student jawab deta hai — Direct Feedback.
Student confused expression deta hai — Moderately Direct Feedback.

Important: Schramm-Osgood Model mein feedback delayed bhi ho sakta hai.

### 8. Berlo’s S-M-C-R Model
David Berlo ne 1960 mein S-M-C-R Model propose kiya. Is model ke chaar main elements hain.

S — Source: Message bhejne wala.
M — Message: Bheji jane wali information.
C — Channel: Message pohanchane ka zariya.
R — Receiver: Message receive karne wala.

Example:
Tum apne dost ko kehte ho, “Kal class hogi.”

Source: Tum.
Message: “Kal class hogi.”
Channel: Tumhari awaaz, jise dost sunta hai.
Receiver: Tumhara dost.

### 9. Factors Affecting Source and Receiver
Berlo’s model ke mutabiq Source aur Receiver ki communication effectiveness par paanch factors asar dalte hain:

1. Communication Skills: Bolne, likhne, sunne aur parhne ki salahiyat.
2. Knowledge: Topic ke bare mein ilm.
3. Attitudes: Kisi shakhs, topic ya situation ke bare mein soch aur rawayya.
4. Social System: Samaji nizam aur social background.
5. Culture: Saqafat, values aur riwayat.

### 10. Communication Skills
Encoding Skills:
- Speaking
- Writing

Decoding Skills:
- Listening
- Reading

Thought/Reasoning:
- Sochna aur daleel ke zariye baat ko samajhna. Yeh communication mein encoding aur decoding dono ko support karta hai.

### 11. Channels in Berlo’s Model
Berlo’s model mein channel ko human senses ke zariye bhi samjhaya jata hai:

1. Seeing — Dekhna
2. Hearing — Sunna
3. Touching — Chhoona
4. Smelling — Soonghna
5. Tasting — Zaiqa chakhna

## Important Names and Years — Quick Revision

- Shannon-Weaver Model: Claude Shannon and Warren Weaver; 1948 is commonly associated with Shannon’s mathematical communication theory.
- Schramm-Osgood Interactive Model: Wilbur Schramm and Charles E. Osgood; 1954.
- Berlo’s S-M-C-R Model: David Berlo; 1960.
