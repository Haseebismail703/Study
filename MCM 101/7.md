# MCM101 — Lesson 7: Message – Root of Communication I

## 1.0 Message – Root of Communication I

### 1.1 Message: Definition and Importance

**Definition:** A message is information or meaning conveyed by a sender to a receiver.

**Roman Urdu:** Message woh information hai jo sender, receiver tak pohanchata hai. Communication mein message important hai kyun ke receiver isi ko samajhne ki koshish karta hai.

**Important Points:**
- Human messages can be complex.
- The same message may be understood differently by different people.
- Clear messages help reduce misunderstanding.
- Mass communication requires special care because the message reaches a large audience.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Sender → Sends the message.
- Receiver → Receives and interprets the message.
- Message → Information or meaning being communicated.

### 1.2 Division of Messages

Messages are discussed in two main categories:

1. Verbal Message
2. Non-verbal Message

**Roman Urdu:** Verbal message mein words use hote hain, jabke non-verbal message mein gestures, signs, symbols aur doosray non-word signals use hote hain.

**MCQ Keywords:** Words → Verbal; gestures and symbols → Non-verbal.

## 2.0 Verbal Message

**Definition:** A message communicated through spoken or written words.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab information words bol kar ya likh kar di jaye, usay verbal message kehte hain.

**Examples:**
- Face-to-face conversation
- Books and newspapers
- Written letters and text messages
- Radio and television speech

**Important MCQ Keywords:**
- Spoken words
- Written words
- Language
- Oral and written communication

### 2.1 Linguistic Barrier

**Definition:** A communication barrier caused by language differences or insufficient language skills.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab sender aur receiver aik doosray ki language ya words ko theek se na samjhein, to message samajhne mein problem hoti hai.

**Example:** A student cannot understand a lecture because it is delivered in a language unfamiliar to the student.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Language differences
- Linguistic skills
- Difficulty understanding words
- Language barrier

**MCQ Trick:** Language samajhne ka masla = Linguistic Barrier.

### 2.2 Standard Meaning’s Problem

**Definition:** A problem that occurs when words do not convey a precise or clearly measurable meaning.

**Roman Urdu:** Kuch words ka meaning clear ya exact nahi hota. Is wajah se sender aur receiver aik hi word ko different tareeqay se samajh sakte hain.

**Example:** “It is hot outside” is less precise than “The temperature is 40°C.”

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Unclear meaning
- Imprecise words
- Exact measurement
- Different interpretations

**MCQ Trick:** “Hot” jaisa vague word aur exact temperature ka difference = Standard Meaning’s Problem.

### 2.3 Written Message Confusion

**Definition:** Confusion that occurs when a written message fails to communicate an experience or feeling clearly to its reader.

**Roman Urdu:** Writer kisi experience ko words mein likhta hai, lekin reader ne woh experience khud na kiya ho to usay writer ki feeling samajhne mein mushkil ho sakti hai.

**Example:** A person who has never visited a mountain may not fully understand another person's written description of the experience.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Written message
- Reader's experience
- Feelings and descriptions
- Difficulty interpreting the message

**MCQ Trick:** Reader ko writer ka experience samajhne mein mushkil = Written Message Confusion.

### 2.4 Static Evaluation

**Definition:** Evaluation of a message or word can be affected by changes in time and place.

**Roman Urdu:** Words aur messages ko samajhne ka tareeqa waqt aur jagah ke saath change ho sakta hai. Is liye context bhi important hota hai.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Time
- Place
- Context
- Changing interpretation

## 3.0 Non-verbal Message

**Definition:** A message communicated through signals other than words, such as gestures, signs, symbols and voice features.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab words ke bajaye body movements, facial expressions, signs, symbols ya awaaz ke utaar-charao se message diya jaye.

**Examples:**
- Facial expressions
- Hand gestures
- Traffic signals
- Symbols
- Voice emphasis

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Without words
- Gestures
- Signs and symbols
- Body language
- Voice accentuation

### 3.1 Gestures

**Definition:** Movements of the hands, face or body used to communicate ideas or feelings.

**Roman Urdu:** Haath, chehre ya body ke isharo se apna message ya feeling convey karna.

**Examples:**
- Victory sign
- Facial expressions
- Wink of an eye
- Hand movements

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Hand movements
- Facial expressions
- Body movements
- Victory sign

**MCQ Trick:** Hand ya face ka ishara = Gesture.

### 3.2 Signs and Symbols

**Definition:** Signs and symbols are recognizable marks or representations used to communicate information or ideas.

**Roman Urdu:** Signs aur symbols aisi alamat hoti hain jinhein dekh kar log kisi message ya meaning ko samajhte hain.

**Examples:**
- Traffic signals
- Red Cross sign
- Ambulance warning signals
- Dove as a symbol of peace

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Signs
- Symbols
- Recognizable marks
- Traffic signals
- Cultural meaning

**MCQ Trick:** Kisi recognizable sign ya symbol se message mile = Signs and Symbols.

### 3.3 Cultural Conflict

**Definition:** A misunderstanding that occurs when different cultures interpret the same gesture or symbol differently.

**Roman Urdu:** Aik culture mein koi gesture normal ya positive ho sakta hai, lekin doosray culture mein uska meaning different ho sakta hai.

**Examples:**
- A gesture may be polite in one culture but offensive in another.
- A symbol may have different meanings in different societies.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Different cultures
- Different meanings
- Cultural differences
- Misunderstanding

**MCQ Trick:** Same gesture, different cultural meanings = Cultural Conflict.

### 3.4 Body Gestures (Language)

**Definition:** Body movements used to express meaning or support a message.

**Roman Urdu:** Body movements ke zariye meaning convey karna ya spoken message ko support karna.

**Example:** Saying “Come here” while moving your hand towards yourself.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Body movements
- Hand gestures
- Non-verbal expression
- Supporting a spoken message

**MCQ Trick:** Body movement se message convey ya support karna = Body Gestures.

### 3.5 Voice Accentuation

**Definition:** Emphasizing words or changing voice level to convey a particular meaning or feeling.

**Roman Urdu:** Awaaz ko loud, soft ya kisi khaas word par stress karke message ka meaning ya emotion clear karna.

**Examples:**
- A speaker raises their voice during a speech.
- A radio presenter stresses an important word.
- A speaker changes tone to express excitement or seriousness.

**MCQ Keywords:**
- Voice level
- Stress on words
- Emphasis
- Tone
- Speech delivery

**MCQ Trick:** Awaaz ki loudness ya word par stress = Voice Accentuation.

## 5.0 Quick Revision — Important MCQ Points

- **Message:** Information or meaning conveyed by a sender to a receiver.
- **Two message categories:** Verbal and Non-verbal.
- **Verbal Message:** Spoken or written words.
- **Linguistic Barrier:** Language-related difficulty.
- **Standard Meaning’s Problem:** Words do not provide a precise meaning.
- **Written Message Confusion:** Difficulty interpreting written descriptions or experiences.
- **Non-verbal Message:** Communication through non-word signals.
- **Gestures:** Hand, face or body movements.
- **Signs and Symbols:** Recognizable marks or representations.
- **Cultural Conflict:** Different interpretations across cultures.
- **Body Gestures:** Body movements that communicate or support meaning.
- **Voice Accentuation:** Stress or changes in voice level.

## 6.0 Most Important MCQ Strategy

1. Sender and receiver → Message.
2. Spoken or written words → Verbal Message.
3. Language difficulty → Linguistic Barrier.
4. Vague wording versus precise measurement → Standard Meaning’s Problem.
5. Difficulty understanding a written experience → Written Message Confusion.
6. Hand or facial movement → Gestures.
7. Recognizable signs or symbols → Signs and Symbols.
8. Different cultural interpretations → Cultural Conflict.
9. Body movement communicating meaning → Body Gestures.
10. Voice stress or loudness → Voice Accentuation.
