# MCM101 — Lesson 1: Mass Communication – An Overview

## 1. Communication

**Easy English Definition:** Communication is the process of sending a message from one person to another.

**Roman Urdu:** Communication ka matlab apna message, information ya feelings doosre insan tak pohanchana hai.

**Example:** A teacher explains a lesson to students.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- Communication means sharing information or messages.
- Sender: The person who sends the message.
- Receiver: The person who receives the message.
- Message: The information being shared.
- Channel: The medium used to send a message.

## 2. Basic Concepts of Communication

**Easy English Definition:** Communication means sharing ideas, thoughts and feelings with others to create understanding.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab hum apne ideas aur feelings doosron ke saath share karte hain taake woh hamari baat samajh saken, isay communication kehte hain.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- The word communication comes from the Latin word **COMMUNIS**.
- COMMUNIS means **common** or **to create commonness**.
- Communication is one of the oldest characteristics of human life.

### Early Forms of Communication

**Gestures:** Communicating through signs or movements.

**Body Expressions:** Showing feelings through facial expressions and body movements.

**Speech:** Communicating through spoken sounds and words.

**Language:** A system of communication in which sounds and gestures have specific meanings.

**Roman Urdu:** Pehle log isharo aur body expressions se apni baat samjhate thay. Baad mein speech aur language ne communication ko zyada asaan banaya.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- Early humans used gestures and body expressions.
- Speech and language helped communication develop.
- Communication helps pass experiences from one generation to another.

## 3. Why Do We Need Communication?

### 3.1 Survival

**Easy English Definition:** Communication helps people meet their needs and survive.

**Roman Urdu:** Insan apni zarooriyat poori karne aur zindagi guzarne ke liye communication karta hai.

### 3.2 Cooperation

**Easy English Definition:** Cooperation means working together to achieve a purpose.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab log mil kar kisi kaam ko complete karte hain, isay cooperation kehte hain.

**Example:** Students work together on a project.

### 3.3 Relationships

**Easy English Definition:** Relationships are connections between people that communication helps build and maintain.

**Roman Urdu:** Communication se log ek doosre ko samajhte aur apne talluqaat mazboot karte hain.

### 3.4 Persuasion

**Easy English Definition:** Persuasion means influencing someone through communication.

**Roman Urdu:** Apni baat samjha kar kisi ki soch ya faislay par asar dalna persuasion hai.

**Example:** A teacher encourages students to study.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- Survival helps people meet their needs.
- Cooperation means working together.
- Communication helps build and maintain relationships.
- Persuasion means influencing others.

## 4. Major Fields of Mass Communication

### 4.1 General Information

**Easy English Definition:** General information provides people with news, views, current affairs and other useful information.

**Roman Urdu:** Is field mein logon tak news, current affairs, sports aur educational information pohanchai jati hai.

**Examples:** TV news, sports updates and educational programmes.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- Main purpose: To inform people.
- Key terms: News, views and current affairs.

### 4.2 Public Relations (PR)

**Easy English Definition:** Public relations means maintaining good relationships between an organization and its public.

**Roman Urdu:** PR ka matlab hai kisi organization ka public ke saath achay talluqaat maintain karna.

**Example:** A university shares official updates with students.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- PR stands for **Public Relations**.
- Main focus: Relationships between an organization and its public.

### 4.3 Publicity / Advertising

**Easy English Definition:** Advertising is a form of communication used to promote products or services, usually for commercial purposes.

**Roman Urdu:** Advertising ke zariye products ya services ki promotion ki jati hai, aam tor par business faiday ke liye.

**Example:** A company advertises a mobile phone on television.

⭐ **IMPORTANT FOR EXAM**
- Advertising promotes products or services.
- It is associated with commercial purposes.
- Modern communication technology helps advertising reach large audiences.

## 5. Important Differences

| Field | Main Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| General Information | To inform people | News programme |
| Public Relations (PR) | To maintain public relationships | University announcement |
| Advertising | To promote products or services | Mobile phone advertisement |
