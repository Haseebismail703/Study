# MCM101 – Lecture 9: Effects of Communication

## 1. Discover

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication helps people discover new things, ideas, information, and other people.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication se hum naye logon, ideas aur information ke baare mein jaante hain.

**Example:** Two people meet and ask each other's names and city.

**MCQ Important:** Immediate effect of communication = Discover.

## 2. Relationships

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication helps people create new relationships and strengthen existing relationships.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication se naye relationships bante hain aur purane relationships mazboot hote hain.

**Example:** Two people become friends through communication.

**Important Points:**
- Personal relationships between people.
- Diplomatic relationships between countries.

**MCQ Important:** Communication creates and strengthens relationships.

## 3. Helping Out Others

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication helps people provide assistance to others and save lives during emergencies.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication ki madad se hum doosron ki help kar sakte hain aur emergency mein jaan bhi bacha sakte hain.

**Example:** Calling a rescue team when a house catches fire.

**Important Points:**
- Asking for help during emergencies.
- Alerting people about danger.
- Helping during natural disasters.

**Important Examples:**
- Hurricanes
- Earthquakes
- Floods
- Excessive rains

## 4. Stimulation / Persuasion

**Easy English Definition:**

Stimulation is the effect of communication that motivates or persuades people to take action.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication logon ko motivate ya persuade karti hai taake woh koi kaam karne ke liye tayyar hon.

**Example:** A teacher motivates students to work harder.

**Important Words:**
- Stimulation = Motivation
- Persuasion = Convincing someone
- Encourage = Himmat dena
- Advice = Mashwara dena

**MCQ Important:** Motivation and persuasion are related to Stimulation.

## 5. Relaxation

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication provides relaxation by reducing stress and changing people's mood.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication se insaan ka stress kam hota hai aur mood fresh hota hai.

**Examples:**
- Listening to jokes.
- Watching funny TV dramas.
- Watching favourite sports matches.
- Talking with friends to reduce tension.

**MCQ Important:** Reducing stress and tension = Relaxation.

## 6. Social Growth

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication helps society grow by sharing knowledge, ideas, and social values.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication se log knowledge, ideas aur experiences share karte hain. Is se society develop hoti hai.

**Important Points:**
- Sharing knowledge and ideas.
- Adopting new technology.
- Adopting better lifestyles.
- Improving society.

**Example:** People share their knowledge and skills with others to improve society.

**MCQ Important:** Development of society through communication = Social Growth.

## 7. Communication Effects in Time and Space

**Easy English Definition:**

Communication helps ideas and knowledge travel across different times and distances.

**Roman Urdu:**

Communication ki wajah se purane zamane ke ideas future generations tak pohanchte hain. Door rehne wale log bhi information share kar sakte hain.

**Important Points:**
- Old ideas remain useful for future generations.
- Old technology can help people today.
- Information can travel to distant places.
- Communication is not limited by time and distance.

**Examples:**
- Scientists share their research with future generations.
- Scientists in different countries share information.

**MCQ Important:** Sharing knowledge across generations and distances = Communication Effects in Time and Space.

# 3/3 Marks Exam Answer

**Question: Explain the effects of communication.**

Communication has many important effects on human life and society. The major effects are:

1. **Discover:** It helps people discover new things, ideas and information.
2. **Relationships:** It helps create new relationships and strengthen existing relationships.
3. **Helping Out Others:** It helps people assist others and save lives during emergencies.
4. **Stimulation:** It motivates and persuades people to take action.
5. **Relaxation:** It reduces stress and provides entertainment.
6. **Social Growth:** It develops society through sharing knowledge, technology and social values.
7. **Time and Space:** It helps transfer ideas and knowledge across time and distance.

**Exam Tip:** Write an introduction, all seven headings and one-line explanations.
