# MCM101 – Lecture 10: Communication and Culture

## 1. Culture

**Easy English Definition:**

Culture is the way of life of people living in a particular society or area.

**Roman Urdu:**

Culture ka matlab hai kisi society ya area ke logon ka rehne aur zindagi guzarne ka tareeqa.

**Culture Includes:**
- Living habits
- Eating and cooking style
- Dressing style
- Language
- Social values
- Traditions
- Religion

**Example:** Pakistani, Sindhi, Punjabi and Balochi cultures have different traditions and lifestyles.

**MCQ Important:** Culture = Way of life.

## 2. Enculturation

**Easy English Definition:**

Enculturation is the process of passing culture from one generation to the next.

**Roman Urdu:**

Enculturation ka matlab hai apni culture aur traditions ko ek generation se doosri generation tak transfer karna.

**Example:**

Parents teach their children:
- Language
- Religion
- Eating habits
- Dressing style
- Traditions

**Important MCQ:**
- Enculturation = Passing culture to the next generation.
- Culture is generally learned from parents and family.

## 3. Acculturation

**Easy English Definition:**

Acculturation is the process of learning the rules and norms of a different culture.

**Roman Urdu:**

Acculturation ka matlab hai kisi doosri culture ke rules, traditions aur lifestyle ko seekhna aur adopt karna.

**Example:**

A Pakistani person moves to America and learns American lifestyle and social practices.

**Important Points:**
- Learning another culture.
- Adopting different social rules.
- Adjusting to a new society.

**MCQ Important:**
- Acculturation = Learning another culture.
- Enculturation = Learning your own culture.

## 4. Cultural Shock

**Easy English Definition:**

Cultural shock occurs when a person faces a completely different culture and finds it difficult to adjust.

**Roman Urdu:**

Jab koi insaan doosre country ya society mein jata hai aur wahan ka lifestyle, traditions aur rules uske apne culture se bohat different hote hain, to usay cultural shock hota hai.

**Example:**

A Pakistani family moves to a Western country and finds its lifestyle and social values very different.

**Important MCQ:**

Cultural Shock = Difficulty in adjusting to a different culture.

## 5. Principles of Intercultural Communication

Intercultural communication means communicating with people from different cultures.

**Roman Urdu:**

Jab hum different cultures ke logon ke saath communication karte hain, usay intercultural communication kehte hain.

### A. Prepare Yourself

**Easy English:**

Learn about another culture before communicating with its people.

**Roman Urdu:**

Doosri culture ke logon se milne se pehle unke traditions aur social rules ke baare mein seekho.

**Example:** Learn about another country's traditions before travelling there.

### B. Overcoming Fears of Unseen

**Easy English:**

People should overcome their fear of communicating with people from different cultures.

**Roman Urdu:**

Different culture ke logon se baat karne ka dar khatam karna chahiye.

**Example:** A tourist should feel confident when talking to local people.

### C. Know Different Meanings Due to Change in Culture

**Easy English:**

Words, gestures and body language may have different meanings in different cultures.

**Roman Urdu:**

Ek culture mein kisi gesture ka jo meaning hota hai, doosri culture mein uska meaning different ho sakta hai.

**Example:** Eye contact can show respect in one culture but disrespect in another.

### D. Knowing Some Hard and Fast Values

**Easy English:**

People should understand the important traditions and fixed values of different cultures.

**Roman Urdu:**

Doosri culture ke important rules aur traditions ka khayal rakhna chahiye.

**Examples:**
- Some Muslims avoid eating with the left hand.
- In Indonesian culture, direct eye contact with elders may be considered disrespectful.
- In American culture, avoiding eye contact may show disinterest.

**Important MCQ:** Cultural values can change the meaning of gestures.

### E. World Moving Towards Acculturation

**Easy English:**

Modern technology, electronic media and travelling help people understand and adopt different cultures.

**Roman Urdu:**

Internet, TV aur travelling ki wajah se log doosri cultures ko samajh rahe hain aur unke lifestyle ke baare mein seekh rahe hain.

**Important Points:**
- Electronic media
- Internet chatting
- Travelling
- Understanding different cultures

## Enculturation vs Acculturation

| Enculturation | Acculturation |
|---|---|
| Learning your own culture | Learning another culture |
| Culture passes between generations | Learning different cultural rules |
| Parents teach children | People adjust to another society |

**Easy Trick:**

- En = Existing / Own culture
- Ac = Another culture

## Culture vs Cultural Shock

- Culture = Way of life.
- Cultural Shock = Difficulty adjusting to a different culture.

# 3/3 Marks Exam Answers

## Q1. What is Culture?

Culture is the way of life of people living in a particular society. It includes their language, religion, traditions, eating habits, dressing style and social values.

## Q2. What is Enculturation?

Enculturation is the process of passing culture from one generation to the next. Parents teach their children language, religion, traditions and living habits.

## Q3. What is Acculturation?

Acculturation is the process of learning the rules and norms of a different culture. It helps people adjust to a new society.

## Q4. What is Cultural Shock?

Cultural shock occurs when a person faces a completely different culture and finds it difficult to adjust to its lifestyle and social values.

## Q5. What are the Principles of Intercultural Communication?

1. Prepare Yourself.
2. Overcoming Fears of Unseen.
3. Know Different Meanings Due to Change in Culture.
4. Knowing Some Hard and Fast Values.
5. World Moving Towards Acculturation.
