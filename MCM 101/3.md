# MCM101 — Lesson 3
## SEVEN CENTURIES OF MASS COMMUNICATION – FROM PRINTING TO COMPUTER

## 1. Newspapers/ magazines

**Easy Definition:** Newspapers and magazines are print media used to share news and information with people.

### Important Years and Names

| Year | Important Event |
|---|---|
| 1621 | The Corrant — London |
| 1631 | The Gazette — France |
| 1690 | Publick Occurrences — Boston, America |
| 1702 | Daily Courant — founded by Samuel Buckley |
| 1803 | Sydney Gazette and the New South Wales Advertiser — Australia |
| 1884 | Linotype machine — Ottmar Mergenthaler |

**Important Keywords**
- **Circulation:** Number of copies distributed.
- **Readership:** People who read a newspaper.
- **Regular publications:** Publications issued at regular intervals.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing ki wajah se books aur newspapers zyada logon tak pohanchne lage. Newspapers ne news aur information regularly dena shuru ki.

## 2. Business side

**Easy Definition:** Advertising is a major source of income for newspapers.

### Important Points
- Newspapers earn a major part of their income through advertising.
- Higher circulation can attract more advertisers.
- Some newspapers target readers with higher incomes.
- Online newspapers may offer free or paid content.
- Online archives contain old articles and records.

**Important Keywords**
- **Advertising:** Paid promotion of products or services.
- **Demographics:** Audience characteristics, such as age and income.
- **Archives:** Collection of old records and articles.

**Roman Urdu:** Newspaper companies advertisements ke zariye paisa kamati hain. Zyada readers advertisers ko attract karne mein madad kar sakte hain.

## 3. Radio – radical change in mass communication

**Easy Definition:** Radio is a medium that communicates information through sound and voice.

### Important Name and Year
- **Guglielmo Marconi:** Associated with the development of wireless radio communication.
- **1895:** Important year associated with Marconi's radio experiments.

**Important Keywords**
- **Radio waves:** Waves used to transmit signals wirelessly.
- **Wireless communication:** Communication without a physical wire between transmitter and receiver.
- **Broadcasting:** Sending information to a large audience.

**Roman Urdu:** Radio ne logon ko door se news, music aur information sunne ka mauqa diya.

## 4. Marconi – transmits signals by radio waves

### Important Names and Facts
- **Guglielmo Marconi:** Inventor and wireless communication pioneer.
- **1895:** Associated with his early radio transmission experiments.
- **Marconi Wireless Telegraph Company:** Company established by Marconi.

**Easy Definition:** Radio waves carry signals through the air without requiring a physical wire.

**Roman Urdu:** Marconi ne wireless signals ki transmission ko develop karne mein important role ada kiya.

## 5. Brief Radio history

### Important Year
- **1920:** Regular radio broadcasting in the USA included presidential election returns.

### Important Points
- Radio became popular for news, music and information.
- More radio stations created competition for available frequencies.
- Too many stations caused signal interference.
- Governments introduced regulations and licensing.

**Important Keywords**
- **Frequency:** A property of a radio signal used to distinguish transmissions.
- **Interference:** When signals disturb one another.
- **Radio receiver:** Device that receives radio signals.
- **Licence:** Official permission to operate a radio station.

**Roman Urdu:** Radio stations barhne ki wajah se frequencies ka masla paida hua aur government ko rules banane pade.

## 6. Broadcast Advertising

### Important Year and Figures
- **1922:** Early paid radio advertising example in New York.
- **10 minutes:** Duration of the promotional talk.
- **$50:** Amount charged for the broadcast.
- **Jackson Heights, New York:** Apartments being promoted.

**Important Term**
- **Toll broadcast:** An early form of paid radio promotion, similar to a commercial advertisement.

**Roman Urdu:** Radio stations paid advertisements chala kar income earn kar sakte hain.

## 7. BBC and CBC

### Important Names and Year
- **BBC:** British Broadcasting Corporation.
- **1923:** BBC established.
- **CBC:** Canadian Broadcasting Corporation.

### Important Points
- BBC used a public licence-fee funding model rather than relying mainly on commercial advertising.
- Canada's broadcasting system also developed along similar public-service lines.
- English and French programming was important in Canada.

**Roman Urdu:** BBC Britain ka broadcasting organization hai. Canada mein English aur French dono languages mein programs ki zaroorat thi.

## 8. Government Regulation

### Important Years and Names
- **1927:** Radio Act in the USA; Federal Radio Commission (FRC) established.
- **1934:** Federal Communications Commission (FCC) replaced the FRC.

### Full Forms
- **FRC:** Federal Radio Commission.
- **FCC:** Federal Communications Commission.

### Important Functions
- Licensing radio transmitters.
- Assigning frequencies to radio stations.
- Regulating communication services.
- FCC's responsibilities included telephone and telegraph services, with television regulation developing later.

**Roman Urdu:** FRC radio stations ko licences aur frequencies assign karta tha. 1934 mein FCC ne uski jagah li aur regulatory responsibilities zyada broad ho gayin.

## 9. Television – miracle in modern mass communication

### Important Years
- **1927:** Important year associated with early television development.
- **November 1964:** Pakistan's first television station opened in Lahore.

### Important Points
- Television combines sound and moving pictures.
- World War II slowed television development.
- Television expanded rapidly after the war.
- Colour television and remote controls became more common later.

**Important Place:** Lahore, Pakistan.

**Roman Urdu:** TV radio se different hai kyun ke is mein awaaz ke saath moving pictures bhi hoti hain.

## 10. Computer

### Important Periods
- **Early 1960s:** Computers used on a limited scale for data processing.
- **1990s:** Computers and the Internet became major sources of mass communication.

### Important Points
- Computers process and store information.
- The Internet enables worldwide communication.
- Online news, websites, email, pictures and videos are examples of digital communication.

**Important Keywords**
- **Data processing:** Organizing and processing information.
- **Internet:** A worldwide network connecting computers and devices.
- **Digital communication:** Sharing information through digital technology.

**Roman Urdu:** Computer aur Internet ne information ko duniya bhar mein tezi se share karna asaan bana diya.
