## Press Freedom, Laws and Ethics

### 1. Press Freedom

**Definition:** Freedom of newspapers and journalists to publish news, opinions and information without unnecessary interference.

Roman Urdu: Media ko news publish karne aur government par criticism karne ki azadi.

**Important:** Press Freedom is not absolute; it is subject to reasonable legal restrictions.

### 2. Press Laws

Press Laws are rules and regulations made to control and guide newspapers, printing and publication.

Roman Urdu: Media ko responsible banane aur misuse rokne ke liye banaye gaye qawaneen.

### 3. Censorship

Censorship means controlling or restricting information.

- Restrictive Censorship: Publication se pehle information rokna.
- Corrective Censorship: Publication ke baad action lena.

### 4. Freedom of Information (FOI)

**Definition:** Right of citizens to access information held by government bodies.

Roman Urdu: Awam ko government records aur information hasil karne ka haq.

**Important Countries and Years:**

| Country | Law / Year |
|---|---|
| Sweden | Freedom of Press Act – 1766 |
| USA | FOI Act – 1966 |
| Australia | FOI Act – 1982 |
| Pakistan | FOI Ordinance – 2002 |
| India | RTI Act – 2005 |

**Important Facts:**
- Pakistan FOI Ordinance introduced by Pervez Musharraf in October 2002.
- Pakistan: Response within 21 days.
- India RTI: Effective 12 October 2005.
- India: Normal response 30 days; life and liberty cases 48 hours.
- PIO = Public Information Officer.

### 6. Article 19 – Constitution of Pakistan 1973

**Definition:** Article 19 guarantees freedom of speech, expression and press, subject to reasonable restrictions imposed by law.

Roman Urdu: Pakistani citizens ko bolne, apni rai dene aur press ki azadi deta hai, lekin qanooni restrictions ke saath.

**Important Restrictions:**
- Glory of Islam
- Security and defense of Pakistan
- Friendly relations with foreign states
- Public order
- Decency and morality
- Contempt of court
- Defamation
- Incitement to an offence

### 7. Article 19 – Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)

Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression.

Is mein information ko seek, receive aur impart karne ka haq bhi shamil hai.

**Difference:**
- Pakistan Constitution Article 19 = Freedom of speech and press.
- UDHR Article 19 = Freedom of opinion and expression.

### 8. Press Council of Pakistan

Press Council ka purpose responsible journalism aur professional ethics ko promote karna hai.

**Important Code of Ethics:**
- Honesty – Imaandari.
- Accuracy – Durust information.
- Objectivity – Baghair bias.
- Fairness – Insaf.
- Privacy – Private life ka respect.
- Confidentiality – Raaz ki hifazat.
- Plagiarism – Doosre ka content apna banana.
- Sensationalism – News ko unnecessarily dramatic banana.

### 9. Declaration of Principles on the Conduct of Journalists

**Very Important:**
- Adopted by: International Federation of Journalists (IFJ).
- First adopted: 1954.
- Place: Bordeaux.
- Amended: 1986.
- Place: Helsingør.

**Important Principles:**
1. Respect for truth.
2. Honest collection and publication of news.
3. Do not hide essential information.
4. Do not falsify documents.
5. Use fair methods to obtain news.
6. Correct inaccurate information.
7. Protect confidential sources.
8. Avoid discrimination.
9. Avoid plagiarism and bribery.

### 10. Important Media Ethics

- Balanced Reporting: Dono sides ki baat ko fair representation dena.
- Neutrality: Personal bias ke baghair reporting.
- Privacy: Victim ki identity aur personal information protect karna.
- No Propaganda: Public ko mislead na karna.
- No Personal Attacks: Kisi ki personal insult ko news na banana.
