# MCM101 — Lesson 2
## Early Mass Communication and Printing Technology

**Course:** Introduction to Mass Communication  
**Lesson:** 02  
**Revision:** Easy English + Roman Urdu  
**MCQs:** Not included

## 1. Mass Communication

**Easy Definition:** Mass communication is sending messages to a large number of people using technology.

**Roman Urdu:** Technology ki madad se ek hi message bohat se logon tak pohanchana mass communication hai.

**Example:** A news programme on television.

⭐ **Important for Quiz**
- Mass communication reaches a large audience.
- Technology helps send messages to many people.
- Early forms included letters, leaflets, books and printing.

## 2. Letters and Leaflets

**Easy Definition:** Letters and leaflets were early ways of sharing written messages with groups of people.

**Roman Urdu:** Purane zamane mein kings ke courts se letters likh kar nobles aur important logon ko bheje jate thay.

⭐ **Important Points**
- Letters were sent from the courts of kings.
- They were sent to nobles and notables.
- They carried messages of common interest.
- They were an early attempt to reach a wider audience through written words.

**Key Words**
- **Nobles:** People of high social rank.
- **Leaflets:** Short printed sheets that share information.
- **Primitive:** An early or simple form.

## 3. Books

**Easy Definition:** Books are written materials used to share knowledge and ideas.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing se pehle books haath se likhi jati thin. In mein religion, society, philosophy, health aur science ke topics hote thay.

⭐ **Important Points**
- Handwritten books existed for centuries.
- They spread religious, social and scientific ideas.
- The central church in Rome employed clerics to copy the Bible by hand.
- Handwritten books helped spread ideas across countries.

**Key Words**
- **Handwritten:** Written by hand.
- **Clerics:** Religious officials.
- **Philosophy:** The study of basic questions about life and knowledge.
- **Scientific advancements:** Progress in science.

## 4. Printing

**Easy Definition:** Printing is a process used to produce many copies of written material.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing se ek hi text ki bohat si copies ban sakti hain, is liye information zyada logon tak pohanchana asaan hua.

⭐ **Important Points**
- Printing was a major breakthrough in mass communication.
- A printing press is a mechanical device used to print copies of text.
- Printing made it easier to reproduce written material.
- The handout identifies China as the place where the printing press was first invented in **1041**.
- Johann Gutenberg developed the printing press as known in the West during the **1440s**.

## 5. Development of the Printing Press

### A. Block Printing

**Easy Definition:** Block printing uses carved blocks to print text or images on paper.

**Roman Urdu:** Is method mein lakri ke block par text ya design carve kiya jata tha, phir usay paper par print kiya jata tha.

⭐ **Important Points**
- Block printing is considered an early printing method.
- It is believed to have originated in China.
- A separate carved block was needed for each page.

### B. Diamond Sutra

**Easy Definition:** The Diamond Sutra is a Buddhist religious text and an early example of printed material.

**Roman Urdu:** Diamond Sutra ek Buddhist religious text hai jo China mein print hua tha.

⭐ **IMPORTANT NAME + YEAR**
- **Name:** Diamond Sutra
- **Type:** Buddhist scripture
- **Place:** China
- **Year:** **868 A.D.**

### C. Movable Type

**Easy Definition:** Movable type uses separate characters that can be arranged to form different texts.

**Roman Urdu:** Is technique mein letters alag alag hote hain. Unhein arrange karke mukhtalif text print kiya ja sakta hai.

⭐ **IMPORTANT NAME + YEAR**
- **Inventor:** Bi Sheng
- **Country:** China
- **Year:** **1041 A.D.**
- **Key term:** Movable type

### D. Johann Gutenberg

**Easy Definition:** Johann Gutenberg was a German goldsmith who developed the printing press in the West.

**Roman Urdu:** Gutenberg German goldsmith tha. Us ne printing techniques ko improve kiya aur movable type printing ko Europe mein widespread banane mein important role ada kiya.

⭐ **IMPORTANT NAME + YEARS**
- **Name:** Johann Gutenberg
- **Nationality:** German
- **Profession before printing press:** Goldsmith
- **Period:** 1440s
- **Important contributions:** Improved movable type printing and used oil-based ink.

### E. Laurens Janszoon Coster

**Easy Definition:** Laurens Janszoon Coster was a Dutchman also credited by some with the invention of the printing press.

**Roman Urdu:** Coster Netherlands ka rehne wala tha. Handout ke mutabiq kuch log usay bhi printing press ki invention ka credit dete hain.

⭐ **IMPORTANT NAME**
- **Name:** Laurens Janszoon Coster
- **Nationality:** Dutch
- **Importance:** An alternative candidate credited with the invention of the printing press.

## 6. Paper and Ink

### Oil-Based Ink

**Easy Definition:** Oil-based ink is ink made using oil as part of its composition.

**Roman Urdu:** Gutenberg ko oil-based ink ke early use ka credit diya jata hai.

### Rag Paper

**Easy Definition:** Rag paper is paper made from cloth fibres.

**Roman Urdu:** Handout batata hai ke China se Muslim traders ke zariye paper Europe pohancha.

⭐ **IMPORTANT PLACE + YEAR**
- **Baghdad**
- **Year:** **794 A.D.**
- The handout mentions a paper mill operating in Baghdad as early as 794.

## 7. Impact of Printing

**Easy Definition:** Printing made it possible to produce more copies of books and spread information more widely.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing se books ki copies banana asaan hua aur knowledge ko zyada logon tak pohanchaya ja saka.

⭐ **Important Points**
- Before printing, scribes copied books by hand.
- Hand-copying books was slow and required a lot of work.
- Printing allowed more copies to be produced.
- Gutenberg's press made book production more efficient.
- Printing helped spread ideas and knowledge.

### Scribes

**Easy Definition:** Scribes were people who copied books and documents by hand.

**Roman Urdu:** Printing press se pehle scribes haath se books ki copies banate thay.

### Why Was Printing Important?

1. More copies of books could be produced.
2. Written information could reach more people.
3. Copying books became more efficient.
4. Knowledge and ideas could spread more widely.
