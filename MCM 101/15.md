# Telegraph Does Miracle in Distance Communication

## Introduction (Lecture Overview)

In this lecture, we will study how Telegraph, Telephone and Telex changed distance communication. These inventions made it possible to send messages quickly over long distances without physically carrying them.

**Roman Urdu:** Is lecture mein hum parhenge ke Telegraph, Telephone aur Telex ne door bethay logon tak messages pohanchana kaise easy aur fast banaya.

## 15.1 Telegraph

**Definition:** Telegraph is a communication device that sends messages over long distances through electrical signals.

**Roman Urdu:** Telegraph ek aisa device hai jo electrical signals ke zariye door ke shehron tak messages bhejta hai.

**Important Event – Samuel Morse (1844)**

- Samuel Morse successfully established a telegraph link between Baltimore and Washington DC.
- Date: May 14, 1844.
- First message: "What hath God wrought".
- Telegraph made long-distance communication much faster.
- Messages could be sent through wires instead of physical transportation.

**Roman Urdu:** Samuel Morse ne 1844 mein telegraph ke zariye pehla famous message Baltimore se Washington DC bheja. Is se door tak news bhejna bohat fast ho gaya.

## 15.2 How Telegraph System Came About?

Before the electric telegraph, people used different methods to send messages over long distances.

**Early Methods:**

- Fires
- Smoke Signals
- Drums

**Claude Chappe (1793)**

- Claude Chappe introduced an early telegraph system in France.
- He used towers and signals.
- Signals were transmitted through the positions of crossed arms.

**Charles Morrison (1753)**

- Described a system of 26 wires.
- Each wire represented one letter of the alphabet.
- Electrical charges moved suspended balls at the receiving station.
- This system was never developed into a practical communication system.

**Karl Friedrich Gauss and Wilhelm Weber (1833)**

- Transmitted signals through wires.
- Used sensitive galvanometers to detect signals.

**Charles Wheatstone (1839)**

- Developed a five-needle telegraph.
- Used it to indicate transmitted letters.
- Telegraph connected Liverpool with Manchester.

**Carl Steinheil**

- Developed a telegraph that printed coded messages on a ribbon.

**Roman Urdu:** Telegraph ek din mein invent nahi hua. Pehle smoke signals aur drums use hote thay. Baad mein scientists ne electrical wires ke zariye messages bhejne ke experiments kiye.

## 15.3 Samuel Morse and His Telegraph

Samuel Morse was an American painter who became interested in electrical communication.

- He learned about electromagnets.
- He realized that electrical current could be used to send messages over long distances.
- Joseph Henry helped him develop the technology.
- Morse developed a system using dots and dashes.
- He patented his telegraph in 1837.
- His practical telegraph system was officially demonstrated in 1844.

**Morse Code:** A system that uses dots (.) and dashes (-) to represent letters and numbers.

**Example:**

SOS = ... --- ...

**Roman Urdu:** Morse Code mein letters ko dots aur dashes ki madad se represent kiya jata hai. In signals ke zariye messages send kiye jate thay.

**Important Dates:**

- 1837 – Morse patented his telegraph.
- 1844 – First famous message transmitted.
- 1866 – Europe and the United States became connected through an underwater telegraph cable.

By 1862, approximately 240,000 km of telegraph cable covered the world.

## 15.4 Days of the Morse Code

Morse Code became an important method of sending messages through telegraph systems.

Important points:

- Data was transmitted at approximately 4–6 bits per second during the latter half of the 1800s.
- Telegraph operators used a telegraph key to send signals.
- Electrical current produced clicking sounds at the receiving end.
- Messages were represented through dots and dashes.

**Roman Urdu:** Morse Code ke zariye operators dots aur dashes send karte thay. Receiver in signals ko samajh kar message read karta tha.

## 15.5 Importance of Telegraph for Print Media

Telegraph brought a major change in the newspaper industry.

**Important Uses:**

- Railroads used telegraph for safety and operations.
- Associated Press used it to send news.
- Businesses used it for stock and commodity information.
- General public used it to send personal messages.
- Military used it for communication during wars.

**American Civil War (1861–1865)**

Telegraph helped the military control troop movements and exchange intelligence.

**Roman Urdu:** Telegraph ne newspapers ko bohat faida diya. Reporters door ke shehron se news jaldi bhej sakte thay aur newspapers ko next day important information mil sakti thi.

## 15.6 Telephone in Historic Perspective

**Definition:** Telephone is a communication device that allows people to transmit voice over a distance through electrical signals.

**Roman Urdu:** Telephone ek aisa device hai jis se log door beth kar apni awaz ke zariye baat kar sakte hain.

Before telephones, people used smoke signals, drums and telegraph systems for long-distance communication.

**Johann Philipp Reis (1863)**

- Demonstrated a working electrical voice-transmission system.
- His machine used a vibrating membrane to open and close an electric circuit.

**Alexander Graham Bell (1876)**

- Received a patent for the first operational telephone in 1876.
- His invention revolutionized communication.
- His assistant was Thomas Watson.

**Important Event – June 2, 1875**

- Bell and Thomas Watson were working on a harmonic telegraph.
- An experiment helped Bell understand that sound could be transmitted through an electric wire.

**Roman Urdu:** Bell ne telephone ko practical form di. Is invention ki wajah se log sirf written messages nahi balkay apni awaz bhi door tak bhej sakte thay.

## 15.7 Development of Telephone

Important developments:

**1876 – Alexander Graham Bell**
- First operational telephone patent.

**1878 – Manual Telephone Exchange**
- First manual telephone exchange opened in New Haven, Connecticut.
- It served 21 customers.
- Operators manually connected telephone wires.

**1891 – Automatic Number Calling**
- First automatic number-calling mechanism was introduced.

**1881 – Long-Distance Service**
- Long-distance telephone service became available.
- Initial transmission quality was poor.

**1900 – Loading Coils**
- Bell System workers developed loading coils to reduce voice distortion.

**1912 – Vacuum Tube**
- Vacuum tubes were adapted as telephone amplifiers.
- They improved signal transmission.

**1915 – Transcontinental Telephone**
- A transcontinental telephone line was demonstrated.

**1956 – Submarine Cable**
- An underwater cable was laid across the Atlantic Ocean.
- It enabled transatlantic telephone communication.

**1962 – Communication Satellites**
- Orbiting communication satellites revolutionized telecommunications.

**1980 – Fiber Optic System**
- Fiber-optic technology was introduced and further improved telecommunications.

## 15.8 Background – How Telephone Works

A telephone converts voice into electrical signals and then converts those signals back into sound.

**Working Process:**

1. A person speaks into the mouthpiece.
2. The mouthpiece acts as a microphone.
3. Sound waves create vibrations inside the microphone.
4. These vibrations produce electrical signals.
5. Signals travel through the telephone network.
6. The receiver converts electrical signals back into sound.
7. The other person hears the voice.

**Roman Urdu:** Jab hum phone par baat karte hain toh microphone hamari awaz ko electrical signals mein convert karta hai. Doosri taraf receiver un signals ko dobara awaz mein convert karta hai.

## 15.9 Telex

**Definition:** Telex is a communication system that uses machines to send typed messages over long distances through telegraph networks.

**Roman Urdu:** Telex ek aisa system hai jisme machines ki madad se typed messages door tak bheje jate hain.

**Important Details:**

- Telex systems started developing around 1935.
- Large telegraph companies developed automatic message-routing systems.
- Telex machines used rotary telephone-style dialing.
- They transmitted coded messages.
- Germany introduced the first wide-coverage telex network during the 1930s.
- The network was used for government communication.
- Its speed was approximately 45.5 bits per second.
- Up to 25 telex channels could share one long-distance telephone channel.
- Telex became an economical method of reliable long-distance communication.

**Roman Urdu:** Telex mein telephone ki tarah connection establish hota tha, lekin is par typed messages send kiye jate thay. Yeh businesses aur government communication ke liye useful tha.
