# MCM101 – Lecture 16
## Print Media

### 1. Print Media

**Definition:** Print Media is a form of mass communication that uses printed materials to deliver information to people.

**Roman Urdu:** Print Media ka matlab hai printed cheezon ke zariye information logon tak pohanchana.

**Examples:**
- Newspapers (Akhbar)
- Magazines (Risalay)
- Books (Kitabein)

**Important:** Print Media ki 3 main categories hain:
1. Newspapers
2. Magazines
3. Books

---

### 2. Newspapers

**Definition:** A newspaper is a printed publication that provides news and information to the public.

**Roman Urdu:** Newspaper aik printed akhbar hota hai jo logon ko rozmarra ki khabrein aur information deta hai.

**Newspaper mein important topics:**
- Political Events – Siyasi khabrein
- Crime – Jurm ki khabrein
- Business – Tijarat
- Culture – Saqafat
- Sports – Khel
- Opinions – Logon ki rai
- Weather – Mosam ki information
- Advertisements – Ishtiharat

### Types of Newspapers

1. International Newspaper – Duniya bhar ki khabrein.
2. Weekly Newspaper – Hafte mein aik baar publish hota hai.
3. Sunday Newspaper – Sunday ko publish hota hai.
4. National Newspaper – Poore mulk ki khabrein.
5. Local Newspaper – Kisi specific shehar ya area ki khabrein.

**Exam Point:** Daily Mirror is associated with Alfred Harmsworth.

### 3. Circulation

**Definition:** Circulation refers to the number of copies of a newspaper distributed or sold.

**Roman Urdu:** Circulation ka matlab hai kisi newspaper ki kitni copies logon tak pohanchti ya sell hoti hain.

**Example:** Agar newspaper ki 100,000 copies distribute hoti hain to uski circulation 100,000 hai.

---

### 4. Magazines

**Definition:** A magazine is a periodical publication that contains articles and information on different topics.

**Roman Urdu:** Magazine aik risala hota hai jo regular time intervals par publish hota hai.

### Periodical

**Definition:** A publication that is published at regular intervals.

**Types of Publication Intervals:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Har hafte |
| Biweekly | Har do haftay |
| Monthly | Har mahine |
| Bimonthly | Har do mahine |
| Quarterly | Har teen mahine |

### Types of Magazines

- Art Magazines
- Business Magazines
- Music Magazines
- Computer Magazines
- Children's Magazines
- Health and Fitness Magazines
- History Magazines
- News Magazines
- Online Magazines
- Sports Magazines
- Regional Magazines

### Important Categories of Magazines

**1. Consumer Magazines**

These magazines are published for the general public.

Roman Urdu: Ye magazines aam logon ke liye hoti hain.

Examples: Time, Esquire, Cosmopolitan.

**2. Business Magazines**

These magazines provide information related to business and professional fields.

Roman Urdu: Ye business aur professional logon ke liye hoti hain.

**3. Controlled Circulation**

Free magazines are distributed to selected readers who meet specific criteria.

Roman Urdu: Aisi magazines jo free mein sirf selected logon ko di jati hain.

---

### 5. Books

**Definition:** A book is a collection of written or printed pages bound together.

Roman Urdu: Book pages ka collection hoti hai jinhein aik cover ke andar bind kiya jata hai.

### Important Terms

**1. Manuscripts**

Books or documents written by hand.

Roman Urdu: Printing press se pehle kitabein haath se likhi jati thin.

**2. Scroll**

A long sheet of writing material rolled into a cylinder.

Roman Urdu: Purane zamane mein likhne ke liye lambi sheets ko roll kiya jata tha.

**3. Papyrus**

An ancient writing material made from the papyrus plant, commonly used in Egypt.

Roman Urdu: Egypt mein papyrus plant se writing material banaya jata tha.

**4. Paper**

Paper-making technology developed in ancient China.

Roman Urdu: Paper banane ki technology ka early development China mein hua.

### Manuscripts vs Printed Books

| Manuscripts | Printed Books |
|---|---|
| Haath se likhi jati thin | Machine se print hoti hain |
| Copies banana mushkil tha | Copies banana asaan hai |
| Rare hoti thin | Zyada available hoti hain |
