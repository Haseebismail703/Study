# MCM101 – Lecture 11
# LANGUAGE IN COMMUNICATION

## 1. Introduction to Language in Communication

**Easy English:**

Language is an important tool of communication. It helps people share information, history, religion, science and knowledge through spoken and written words.

**Roman Urdu:**

Language communication ka important tool hai. Iske zariye hum apni information, history, religion, science aur knowledge doosron tak pohanchate hain.

**Important Points:**
- Non-verbal communication is important in real-time communication.
- Language communicates through spoken and written words.
- Historical records and religious teachings are passed through words.
- There are more than 4,000 recognized languages.

## 2. Flaws in Communication

Languages are powerful tools, but they also have some flaws which create problems in communication.

**Four main flaws:**
1. Polarization
2. Labeling
3. Static Meanings
4. Indiscrimination

## 3. Polarization

**Easy English Definition:**

Polarization is the use of either-or language that describes things in extreme positions.

**Roman Urdu:**

Kisi cheez ko sirf do extreme options mein divide karna, jaise good or bad, yes or no. Reality mein beech ke options bhi ho sakte hain.

**Examples:**

- A person is called either good or bad, although he may have both qualities.
- A movie is called good or bad, although its acting may be good and story bad.
- During elections, people are asked to say yes or no, even if they agree with only some policies.

**Important Point:**

Polarization does not allow us to communicate the complete truth.

**MCQ Keyword:** Either-or language / Extreme positions.

## 4. Labeling

**Easy English Definition:**

Labeling means giving people a particular name or category and keeping the same impression about them.

**Roman Urdu:**

Kisi person ya group ko ek label de dena aur phir uske bare mein hamesha wahi impression rakhna.

**Examples:**

- Calling Scots stingy people.
- Calling people of a particular tribe quarrelsome.
- Considering every person from a particular group the same.

**Important Points:**

- Labeling creates assumptions.
- It can lead to prejudice.
- It can create stereotypes and fixed views.

**MCQ Keyword:** Giving labels / Assumptions about people.

## 5. Static Meanings

**Easy English Definition:**

Static meanings mean keeping old meanings or impressions about a person even when that person has changed.

**Roman Urdu:**

Kisi person ke bare mein purani soch ko hamesha yaad rakhna, chahe woh person waqt ke sath change hi kyun na ho gaya ho.

**Examples:**

- A college friend was considered naughty, but after many years he becomes responsible. Friends still call him naughty.
- A person visits another country and gets cheated. He continues to think that everyone from that country is dishonest.

**Important Points:**

- People can change over time.
- Old impressions may remain unchanged.
- Language does not always reflect the current reality.

**MCQ Keyword:** Old impressions / Fixed meanings.

## 6. Indiscrimination

**Easy English Definition:**

Indiscrimination means grouping different people under one common name and assuming that they have the same behaviour.

**Roman Urdu:**

Mukhtalif logon ko ek hi group mein rakh kar samajhna ke sab ka behaviour same hai.

**Examples:**

- Thinking all police officers have the same behaviour.
- Assuming all students behave in the same way.
- Hearing that someone is a model and immediately forming a particular impression about her.

**Important Points:**

- Common nouns group different people together.
- Every person in a group is not the same.
- It creates wrong assumptions.

**MCQ Keyword:** Common nouns / Same behaviour.

## 7. Purpose of Debate

**Easy English Definition:**

The purpose is not to prove that languages are worthless, but to identify areas where language fails to communicate the complete meaning.

**Roman Urdu:**

Is discussion ka maqsad language ko useless prove karna nahi, balkay un problems ko samajhna hai jahan language apna complete meaning convey nahi kar pati.

**Important Point:**

Language is useful, but sometimes it creates misunderstandings and communication problems.

|---|---|
| Either-or language | Polarization |
| Good or Bad | Polarization |

| Old opinion remains unchanged | Static Meanings |

| Assumptions about a particular group | Labeling |

## Quick Revision – Remember These Four

1. **Polarization:** Extreme choices (Good / Bad).
2. **Labeling:** Giving people labels.
3. **Static Meanings:** Keeping old impressions.
4. **Indiscrimination:** Assuming everyone in a group is the same.
